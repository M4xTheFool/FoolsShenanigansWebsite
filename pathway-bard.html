Sequences

Sequence 9: Bard

  • Inspiring Song: Sings hymns that boost allies' courage, strength and recovery.
  • Fearless: Resists fear and helps drive it out of others.
  • Hardy Body: Gains a healthier, sturdier body.

Sequence 8: Light Suppliant

  • Sunshine: Calls down scorching sunlight that burns undead and spirits and dazzles foes.
  • Daytime: Floods an area with light as if it were noon.
  • Blessing: Shields allies against fear, cold, darkness and deathly influence.
  • Sun Eyes: Sees in the dark and senses undead and evil beings nearby.
  • Holy Water: Prepares holy water and performs rituals that call stronger light.

Sequence 7: Solar High Priest

  • Holy Light Summoning: Calls down a column of purifying light on evil.
  • Fire of Light: Conjures dense golden flames that burn away corruption.
  • Cleave of Purification: Blesses weapons and bullets with purifying force.
  • Sun Halo: Radiates a halo that steadies courage and cleanses evil influence.
  • Holy Oath: Swears an oath that boosts its body and holy damage.
  • Horror Immunity: Shrugs off supernatural fear.

Sequence 6: Notary

  • Amplification: Notarizes a power or effect as valid, making it stronger.
  • Nullification: Declares an effect invalid, weakening or dispersing it.
  • Authentication: Judges whether a statement, identity, formula or contract is genuine.
  • Binding Contract: Witnesses agreements so that breaking them brings holy punishment.

Sequence 5: Priest of Light

  • Light of Holiness: Calls down a blazing pillar of light that can vaporize targets.
  • Purification Halo: Fills an area with golden radiance that strengthens allies and destroys impure creatures.
  • Bane of the Unclean: Its light and flame hit undead and corrupted beings especially hard.

Sequence 4: Unshadowed

  • Sunbird Body: Becomes a demigod with an incomplete Sunbird form, far stronger, faster and tougher.
  • Unshadowed Domain: Fills an area with blazing light that reveals hidden things and suppresses darkness.
  • Direct Purification: Removes corruption, parasites, illness and mental influence, and in time can cleanse Beyonder characteristics.
  • Unshadowed Spear: Hurls a spear of concentrated holy light.
  • Flaring Sun: Ignites a fierce miniature sun of searing light.
  • Holy Armor: Wraps itself in protective armor and weapons of light.

Sequence 3: Justice Mentor

  • Personal Justice: Turns its own standard of justice into an order binding it, the divine and the world.
  • Justice Halo: Covers a vast region, empowering acts that fit its justice and weakening those that break it.
  • Judgment of Justice: Focuses that suppression onto a single target.
  • Holy Contract: Forms sacred contracts strong enough to restrain angels.
  • Distant Armor: Extends protective holy armor to allies far away.

Sequence 2: Lightseeker

  • Miniature Sun: Becomes a small sun, pouring out immense heat, light and purification.
  • Light Body: Turns into massless light to move at light speed, scatter to avoid harm and reform.
  • Praise the Sun: Produces absolute purification while strengthening itself.
  • Spear of Light: Seals the surrounding space so enemies cannot escape.
  • Justice Trial: Brutally punishes anyone who violates its core justice.

Sequence 1: White Angel

  • Holy Kingdom: Creates a vast radiant domain where darkness, undead and corruption can barely exist.
  • Pure White Light: Purifies by breaking down anything out of harmony with its holiness.
  • Order and Faith: Draws strength from social order and turns people's awe into faith.
  • Regional Sun: Shines as the sun of a whole region, inspiring and reinforcing followers.

Sequence 0: Sun

  • Sun Incarnate: Can act, see and strike wherever light shines.
  • Eternal Day: Brings endless daylight that exposes concealment and suppresses death and darkness.
  • Divine Contracts: Binds even gods with contracts they cannot easily resist.
  • Inextinguishable Light: Extremely hard to destroy, able to return after seeming annihilation.

Above the Sequence: God Almighty

A Great Old One holds Chaos Sea and the Uniqueness of every pathway it gathers.

Symbolism
Creation, the source of matter and energy, and the place where the real and the illusory merge.
  • Omnipotent and Omniscient: Sees and can do almost anything within the Chaos Sea's reach, as the full title says.
  • Source of Matter and Energy: Draws matter and energy out of the Chaos Sea to make whatever it needs.
  • Real and Illusory: Merges the real and the imagined, so what it pictures can become real.
