Max joins ASG

1630, Second Epoch, Pact or alliance, Eastern Continent

Pact or alliance

What Happened

Max joined the Ancient Sun God and his legion, as a commander and general only below the Ancient Sun God and Medici.
He quickly became really good friends with Amanises. He advanced to Weather Warlock with the help of Ancient Sun God, but rejected the offer to advance to Conqueror saying: It's not yet the time. He was also really good friends with Ouroboros. He became friends with Adam and Amon, with a relationship of mainly mutual respect.
