Backstory

The second transmigrator. The GOAT Emperor. One and only deserving to become the Black Emperor. He was originally just a guy named Huang Tao, but after transmigrating he brought the Era of Steam (and machinery) to the world. His diaries are written in Chinese, so almost no one in this world can read them. In the original novel Klein could. In this story Nika can, since she is half-fluent in Chinese. The pages reach her through the Troupe, and one line stays with her: "Only true gods can protect the people they care about."

Uniqueness

Has absorbed the Uniqueness of the Lawyer pathway.

Abilities

Sequence 9: Savant (Savant pathway)

  • Perfect Recall: Remembers everything they have read, seen or experienced.
  • Engineering Mastery: Quickly grasps machines, firearms and lab equipment, repairing and improving them beyond normal standards.
  • Rapid Learning: Absorbs mathematics, physics, chemistry and technical crafts at remarkable speed.

Sequence 8: Archaeologist (Savant pathway)

  • Historical Insight: Reads an object's age, origin, purpose and dangers from its construction, wear, symbols and spiritual traces.
  • Ruin Lore: Knows ancient languages, lost cultures and the taboos and traps hidden in ruins.
  • Field Hardiness: Gains a sturdy body and survival skills for long expeditions in the wild.

Sequence 7: Appraiser (Savant pathway)

  • Appraisal: Quickly works out the properties, activation and drawbacks of most mystical items without divination.
  • Authentication: Spots forgeries, hidden damage, contamination and an item's true worth.
  • Safe Handling: Uses unfamiliar sealed artifacts while sidestepping much of their negative side effects.

Sequence 6: Artisan (Savant pathway)

  • Crafting: Builds mystical items and Beyonder weapons from characteristics, spiritual materials, symbols and machinery.
  • Artifact Repair: Mends damaged mystical items, changes how they activate and reduces some of their drawbacks.
  • Advanced Machinery: Designs mechanical devices well beyond the technology of the era.

Sequence 5: Astronomer (Savant pathway)

  • Astronomy: Understands celestial phenomena well enough to draw power and guidance from the stars.
  • False Starry Sky: Projects a fake sky of constellations that misleads astrology and other divination.
  • Star of Curses: Condenses starlight into rays that weaken, mutate or injure a target.
  • Star Blessing: Uses absorbed starlight to change gravity for a chosen target or area.
  • Celestial Calculation: Precisely computes trajectories, distances and timing for navigation and targeting.

Sequence 4: Alchemist (Savant pathway)

  • Transmutation: Reshapes and converts materials, alloys and energy with extreme precision.
  • Life Alchemy: Creates artificial life such as golems, mechanical creatures and altered plants, and can give objects limited intelligence.
  • Elixirs: Brews alchemical concoctions that strengthen allies or weaken enemies.

Sequence 3: Arcane Scholar (Savant pathway)

  • Body of Knowledge: Transforms into a form made of knowledge for anywhere from minutes to days.
  • Environment Analysis: Extracts precise information from the surroundings to reconstruct what happened there.
  • Life Absorption: Drains vitality from living things across a vast radius.
  • Disassembly Ray: Fires a beam of light that breaks down the structure of most objects.
  • Arcane Technology: Translates observed powers into reproducible machines, rituals and artifacts.

Sequence 2: Knowledge Magister (Savant pathway)

  • Teaching: Pours knowledge and skills into others, or strips learned skills away from them.
  • Analysis: Breaks any power, object or phenomenon down to its working principles and designs counters to it.
  • Enlightenment: Sparks sudden understanding in a target, granting temporary mastery of a field.
  • Rapid Creation: Turns complex designs into working creations far faster and awakens intelligence in machines.

Sequence 1: Knowledge Emperor (Mystery Pryer pathway)

  • Knowledge as Power: Folds fully understood Beyonder powers into their informational body to produce matching effects.
  • Information Control: Gathers, removes, forges or hides information through the spirit world and mystical links.
  • Overwhelming Teaching: Floods a mind with crushing or useless knowledge.
  • Forbidden Prying: Can probe secrets of gods and Great Old Ones, though at great risk.

Sequence 0: Black Emperor (Lawyer pathway)

  • Authority of Distortion: Twists underlying rules, causality and mystical links, turning entrances into exits or turning hostile power aside.
  • Corruption and Disorder: Spreads corruption and escalating chaos through minds, societies, powers and laws.
  • Autocracy: Makes every hierarchy and rule within their order serve the Emperor alone.
  • Undying Order: Can return from death so long as the order they built and their apotheosis arrangements survive.

Events

  1. 1143
    Roselle Gustav's AwakeningDiscovery
    Northern Continent (Republic of Intis - Trier)
    With Max (Met with him at some point), Roselle Gustav (Awoke), Adam and Amon
    Sealed Artifacts: Second Blasphemy Slate

    The second transmigrator, Huang Tao, awakens in the Kingdom of Intis as Roselle Gustav. Bringing the Industrial Revolution, steam machinery, and modern governance to the Northern Continent, he overthrows the Sauron royal family, establishes the Republic of Intis, and later crowns himself Emperor Caesar. After viewing the Second Blasphemy Slate and joining the Twilight Hermit Order alongside Adam and Amon, he discovers the truth of the Outer Deities on the Crimson Moon and becomes corrupted by the Mother Goddess of Depravity

  2. 1198
    Fall of Emperor RoselleDeath, Battle, Advancement
    Northern Continent (Trier - White Maple Palace)
    With Roselle Gustav (Deceased/Resurrecting), Aucuses, Bernadette Gustav, Adam and Max (Witness at some point)

    To purge the Mother Goddess of Depravity's corruption through death and resurrection, Roselle Gustav secretly switches from Sequence 1: Knowledge Emperor (Savant/Paragon pathway) to Sequence 0: Black Emperor (Lawyer pathway), building nine secret mausoleums. In 1198, besieged at the White Maple Palace by the Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun, the Church of the God of Craftsmanship, and the Sauron family, Roselle completes his apotheosis and immediately perishes, retreating into his last remaining Black Emperor mausoleum to await a future savior who can help him resurrect free of corruption.
