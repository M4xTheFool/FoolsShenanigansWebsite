Sealed Artifact 0-08 (Quill of Alzuhod)

Sealed Artifact A-005, Spectator (Visionary) pathway, Sequence 1 level

Appearance

An ordinary-looking quill pen.

Ability

Works like the Visionary pathway's Sequence 1 Author: what is written with it can steer events in the real world. It writes without ink, and most of its powers are kept secret.

Drawback

It has a will of its own, tries to kill its user and is extremely hard to control.

Notes

In this story Ince's death is Nika's Sequence 4 Director ritual. She directs the performance, Leonard lands the blow for his captain, and Adam is the audience. When it is over he bends down, picks up the quill and leaves, as he did in the original novel. The Church of the Evernight Goddess never gets it back. The novel ties the quill to the Dragon of Imagination Ankewelt, reason enough for the Angel of Imagination to want it. During the ritual the quill tries to write Nika, and As It Was Written doesn't allow it.
