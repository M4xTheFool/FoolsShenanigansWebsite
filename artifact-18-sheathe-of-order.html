Sheathe of Order

Sealed Artifact A-018, Arbiter (Justiciar) pathway, Sequence 2 level

Appearance

Its a simple brown sheathe with the Justiciar symbol on it.

Ability

It was made to Seal and balance the Sword of Entropy.

Drawback

If not with the Sword of Entropy it starts to steal spirituality from the holder until they die.

Notes

When sheathing the Sword of Entropy, all of the drawbacks of both items cancel out, as long as they're withing 10 meters of each other and the sword is sheathed at least for 5 minutes once every 6 hours.
