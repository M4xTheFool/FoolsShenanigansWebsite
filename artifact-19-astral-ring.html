Astral Ring

Sealed Artifact A-019, Apprentice (Door) pathway, Sequence 3 level

Appearance

Silver ring with a black/blue gem, lthat looks like outer space.

Ability

Travel through the Spirit World and the Astral World. It's also the only way out of the Forsaken Land of the Gods, for an Angel or above, and it carries only its wearer.

Drawback

The longer it's worn, the worse the mental and physical exhaustion. More than 15 minutes kills a mid-Sequence Beyonder; tolerance rises with Sequence. Leaving the Forsaken Land takes a huge toll even on Max: his people call out his honorific to hold him together, and he needs a week to recover.

Notes

The Forsaken Land of the Gods is a one-way trip. Going back in is easier: Max's Conqueror authority, all his believers, the Sword of Entropy and the ring together carry him in, and he can bring one person with him at the cost of 3 days of rest. Whoever he brings in can't leave the same way.

Held By
