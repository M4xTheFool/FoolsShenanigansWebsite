An outgoing, calm and really strong individual. People feel safe around him. He likes people and is basically Maxs speaker for the people. He walks around dignified, in a really shiny silver armor, that somehow is almost always covered in blood.

About

A very capable and loyal lieutenant.

Backstory

Raised in the Broken City. Admired Max since childhood and became strong so he could be by his side. He is Maxs most loyal subject, a close friend and someone he can truly rely on. They joke around, sometimes even train together.

Abilities

Sequence 9: Warrior

  • Peak Physique: Gains strength, agility and endurance far beyond ordinary people.
  • Combat Reflexes: Reacts in battle with sharp instincts and quickly learns unfamiliar fighting styles.
  • Weapon Handling: Uses almost any ordinary weapon or armor effectively from the start.

Sequence 8: Pugilist

  • Unarmed Mastery: Masters punching, kicking, grappling and every kind of bare-handed fighting.
  • Greater Strength: Grows strong enough to wrestle larger foes and break restraints while keeping balance.
  • Iron Vitality: Partly resists supernatural effects through sheer vitality and willpower.

Sequence 7: Weapon Master

  • Weapon Mastery: Expertly wields anything used as a weapon, from unfamiliar firearms to mystical items.
  • Weak Point Sense: Senses where an enemy is most vulnerable.
  • Artifact Handling: Lessens the harmful side effects of weapon-shaped sealed artifacts it wields.
  • Improvised Arms: Turns ordinary objects into deadly weapons through understanding of weight, reach and timing.

Sequence 6: Dawn Paladin

  • Dawn Armor: Clads itself in weightless silver armor of dawn light that mends by burning stamina.
  • Sword of Dawn: Condenses holy dawn light into purifying weapons.
  • Hurricane of Light: Shatters a dawn weapon into a storm of radiant blades, deadly to undead, spirits and shadows.
  • Light of Dawn: Sheds sacred light that dispels illusions and weakens shadows, wraiths and corruption.

Sequence 5: Guardian

  • Protection: Plants a weapon to raise an invisible, nearly unbreakable barrier around allies.
  • Shield Bearer: Takes damage meant for the allies it is protecting.
  • Fortress Body: Has such durability that ordinary firearms barely trouble it.
  • Steadfast Mind: Strongly resists illusions and mind control.

Sequence 4: Demon Hunter

  • Insight: Instinctively reads a target's true nature, weaknesses, corruption or possession.
  • Demon Hunting Lore: Knows monsters deeply and crafts oils, potions and ointments that counter them.
  • Hidden Intent: Conceals its intentions and actions from danger sense, divination and prophecy.
  • Demigod Body: Gains demigod strength and an incomplete giant form.

Sequence 3: Silver Knight

  • Silver Giant: Becomes a huge giant clad in silver armor it can sustain indefinitely.
  • Mercury Body: Turns into liquid silver to dodge attacks, bind or smother enemies, or armor an ally.
  • Silver Rapier: Launches nearly invisible blades of light that appear beside or inside targets.
  • Light Travel: Hides inside and moves through sources of light.
