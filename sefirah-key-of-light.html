Above the Sequence

Symbolism
Fate, fortune and endless change.
  • Incarnation of Fate: Embodies fate itself, seeing its course and bending it.
  • Endless Disorder: Turns fortune into misfortune and back again, without end.

Notes

Full title: Key of Light, Endless Disorder, Incarnation of Fate (Ch. 1347). Sealed in the Western Continent in an earlier age, like every Sefirah except Sefirah Castle and the Chaos Sea (Ch. 1369). Sequel: the Buddhist Kingdom of Light, whose will, the Golden Buddha, was never shattered. The symbolism and Great Old One abilities are an interpretation of the full title and the description; the novel never lists them.
