Second Blasphemy Slate

Sealed Artifact A-012, Spectator (Visionary) pathway

Ability

Records the Great Old Ones and the Sefirot, the pathway groups they gather and what each Great Old One needs: its Sefirah, every Uniqueness of the group and a Sequence 1 characteristic of each pathway (Ch. 1345, 1347).

Notes

Adam shows it to Klein in the Corpse Cathedral (Ch. 1345) and uses it to explain the Pillars (Ch. 1347). Its look and any drawback aren't described in the chapters checked for this set, so they're left blank.
