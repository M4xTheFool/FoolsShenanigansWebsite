Quicksilver Crown

Sealed Artifact A-022, Monster (Wheel of Fortune) pathway, Sequence 2 level

Appearance

A crown of liquid silver. Look closely enough and it is a snake eating its own tail.

Ability

The wearer sees about 15-20 seconds into the future, across about a battlefield's width, and can choose the future he favours. He sees what he has to do, his own luck rises, and his enemies' luck falls.

Drawback

Before he can reach the future, the wearer first sees everything bad that could happen to the people he cares about, if they are involved. Low spirituality drain, heavy mental load.

Notes

When not in use, it floats around the head like a liquid. In use, it starts dripping, and the future is reflected in the drops. The wearer can turn it up or down: the more of fate's possible turns he wants to see, the faster it drips and the heavier the load. A gift from Ouroboros from when both served the Ancient Sun God, made from the last Soothsayer characteristic in the Forsaken Land of the Gods.
