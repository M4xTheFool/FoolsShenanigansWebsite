Nika and Max's apothesis

25 June 1360, Battle and Advancement, Northern Continent

Battle and Advancement

What Happened

Nika together with Max waged war and succesfly slain the Lord of the Storms and Eternal Blazing Sun.
The Fight ended by the battlefield being unwound in the Scripts meanwhile Max outside of them consumed his potion and succesfully advanced to Red Priest. Nika succesfully advanced to Maestro. The characteristics and uniquenesses were split evenly (Max got the Tyrant Uniqueness and 1 Sequence One of the Tyrant pathway and two Sun Sequence One., Nika got Sun uniqueness and 1 Sequence One of Sun plus the last 2 Sequence One Tyrant)/ The Primordial Demoness and the Hidden Sage retreated after being wounded. Herabergen retreated severly wounded. Many angels of both pathways were killed during the Event.
