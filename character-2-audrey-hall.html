About

Earl's daughter, 'the most dazzling gem of Backlund', and a Visionary-pathway reader of minds.

Backstory

Audrey Hall is the youngest child and only daughter of a Loen earl who sits in the Cabinet and ranks among the kingdom's leading bankers, and Backlund society calls her its most dazzling gem. In early July 1349 she was pulled onto the Everstage by accident. Hers was the bright, childish mask Nika grabbed on her first night there, and the name that stuck to it was Alice. At the Troupe's second meeting she traded with Odysseus for the Spectator formula and became a Visionary pathway Beyonder, adept at reading and soothing minds. She later joined the Psychology Alchemists. When she met Nika in society as a fellow noble, she soon suspected that the girl who survived the attack on the Marlowes was a Beyonder. Before she could connect the dots, the Prompter took off her mask for her alone. It was a sign of trust from the Phantom and from Nika herself, and it made them real friends. After the Great Smog of Backlund, Audrey threw herself into the Loen Charity Bursary Foundation and pushed her family to back laws that improve workers' conditions. She turned down Max's offer of a trip into the Forsaken Land. By 1360 she has advanced further along the Visionary pathway

    Nika's Awakening & First Curtain CallBattle and Advancement
    Backlund (Marlowe Manor & Empty Theatre)
    With Nika (Awakens), Klein Moretti (dies), Amanises, Viscount Marlowe, Fors Wall, Audrey Hall, Alger Wilson, Emlyn White, Cattleya, Leonard Mitchell, Xio Derecha, Sharron and Derrick Berg

    After Amanises uses Antigonus to divert the Curtain of Fool characteristics into the Forsaken Land of the Gods, the third transmigrator (Zhou Mingrui / Klein Moretti) perishes upon arrival, triggering the release of the fourth cocoon: 22-year-old Polish stage technician Weronika "Nika" Sparrow. Because she bowed on an empty theatre stage at the end of her luck ritual on Earth, the unsealed 10th Sefirah—the Everstage—claims her before the Celestial Worthy's net can bind her to the Fool pathway. She awakens in Backlund in the body of 18-year-old Lady Viola Veronica Marlowe seconds after a Secret Order strike team (a Marionettist, a Magician, and a Clown) raids Viscount Marlowe's manor for a Sequence 4 Bizarro Sorcerer characteristic. Waking in a pool of blood with her fatal head wound healing and a silver-white silk streak in her red hair, Nika grabs a revolver and shoots the Secret Order Clown dead as the Nighthawks arrive. A few nights later, she repeats the ritual in an empty theatre, bows to the dark seats, hears a single clap, and steps onto the Everstage for the first time as the Prompter—recruiting Audrey Hall and Alger Wilson (Odysseus) into the Troupe
