Abilities

Sequence 9: Monster

  • Fate Perception: Senses danger, malice and shifts in luck, sometimes catching a glimpse of what is coming.
  • Strong Intuition: Receives warnings as visions, feelings or sudden urges to avoid a place or action.
  • Spirit Sensitivity: Extremely keen spiritual perception lets them hear and see what others miss.

Sequence 8: Robot

  • Precise Calculation: Computes probabilities, trajectories and patterns with machine-like accuracy.
  • Emotional Control: Suppresses panic and hides reactions, staying coldly rational under pressure.
  • Divination Mastery: Becomes skilled at divination and at shielding themselves from others' divination.
  • Physical Boost: All physical abilities noticeably improve.

Sequence 7: Lucky One

  • Passive Luck: Good fortune arrives on its own, from finding money to enemy attacks going astray.
  • Lucky Breaks: Useful coincidences, timely discoveries and narrow escapes happen far more often than chance allows.

Sequence 6: Calamity Priest

  • Calamity Sense: Detects approaching disasters and misfortune building around people or places.
  • Calamity Attraction: Draws disasters toward their body and unleashes them on enemies, though they can strike the Priest too.
  • Mind Storm: Strikes an enemy's spirit body directly, leaving them sick and disoriented.
  • Calamity Transfer: Ritually shifts part of a coming disaster onto another person, object or place.

Sequence 5: Winner

  • Luck Control: Starts actively steering their own fortune, building it up before battles, gambles or escapes.
  • Misfortune Curse: Places limited bad luck on an enemy, from missed shots to stumbling into danger.
  • Winning Foresight: Senses the best timing and choice, and excels at divination and counter-divination.

Sequence 4: Misfortune Mage

  • Misfortune Field: Curses everyone within a circle of roughly 100 to 300 meters with bad luck.
  • Deadly Misfortune: A stronger curse of ill fortune that can endanger a target's life.
  • Blessing: Grants good luck to one or more targets, paid from their own luck or borrowed from the target's future.
  • Mercury Body: Their body naturally resists divination and prophecy aimed at them.
  • Fate Sense: Reliably feels shifts in fate and the presence of beings of higher rank.

Sequence 3: Chaoswalker

  • Tangled Fate: Perceives the chaotic strands of fate and can steer clear of them or seal them away.
  • Probability Turbulence: Throws plans, attacks and divinations into chaos so outcomes become unpredictable.
  • Spirit Baptism: Purifies a target's spirit, calming its disorder and making it stable.

Sequence 2: Soothsayer

  • Prophecy: Sees branching futures and pivotal choices, delivering high-level prophecies.
  • Fate Authority: Grants fortune, imposes angel-level misfortune or places an opportunity at an exact moment.
  • Fate Links: Senses and uses the connections between things and a person's fate.
  • Dreams and Mirrors: Reaches through the spirit world to influence dreams and mirrors.

Sequence 1: Snake of Mercury

  • Reboot: Resets a target or area to an earlier state, healing wounds or undoing recent events.
  • Reincarnation: Escapes death by restarting their own life cycle through a prepared rebirth.
  • River of Fate: Perceives and manipulates the currents of fate linking people, events and eras.

From the Uniqueness: Sequence 0: Wheel of Fortune

  • Authority of Fate: Rewrites how causes, choices and outcomes connect, granting great fortune or cascading failure.
  • Divine Reboot: Resets vast regions, events or states to an earlier point in a cycle.

Sealed Artifacts

Die of Probability

Details
A-023, Monster (Wheel of Fortune) pathway, Sequence 0 level

It looks like an ordinary die with red pips on each of its faces.

Ability
At 1, the probability of adversely affecting the target is amplified to the limit. Everything you do will fail and rare unlucky events become extremely likely and common.
At 2, the unfavorable probability is greatly increased. This does not mean that 2 must be safer than 1 because once the probability becomes higher, any accident can happen.
3 amplifies unfavorable probabilities.
4 to 6 are the opposite.
Drawback
It is uncontrollable. Even when undisturbed and left in a sealed container, the face can randomly change.
It has a living nature, always oscillating between unlucky and lucky for the user. If you don’t pay attention, you will die by accident. Moreover, it can also manipulate the probability of behavior for the people and things around the user.
It is suspected that given a long enough time, it can affect the whole world, and the behavior of all beings will be determined by the number shown, of course, except the gods.
