Sequences

Sequence 9: Assassin

  • Shadow Concealment: Melts into darkness and shadow to stay hidden, though sprinting breaks the cover.
  • Light Feet: Gains agile limbs, silent footwork and speed suited to stealth and evasion.
  • Feather Fall: Makes the body extremely light to drop safely from great heights or glide.
  • Mighty Blow: Gathers all the body's strength into a single devastating strike.
  • Heightened Senses: Sharper hearing and vision, including the ability to see in the dark.

Sequence 8: Instigator

  • Instigation: Plants thoughts in people's minds, nudging them toward quarrels, betrayals or other chosen actions.
  • Emotion Sensing: Reads the emotions and desires stirring in others to find levers for manipulation.
  • Physical Enhancement: The body grows somewhat stronger, though combat skill barely improves.

Sequence 7: Witch

  • Witch's Change: The potion turns a male drinker into a woman and greatly heightens beauty and charm.
  • Black Flame: Conjures black fire that burns spirituality, striking spirit bodies and burning even underwater.
  • Frost: Commands ice to seal locks, slick floors, freeze foes and send out chilling winds.
  • Mirror Substitution: Uses a mirror to make a stand-in that takes fatal harm in the Witch's place.
  • Curse: Harms a target from afar through a medium such as their blood, hair or a doll.
  • Witchcraft: Turns invisible with powder and incantation, hides briefly inside mirrors and excels at mirror divination.

Sequence 6: Pleasure

  • Silk Threads: Spins near-invisible threads from hair to bind enemies, sense movement and weave cocoons.
  • Pleasure: Floods victims with overwhelming pleasure through threads or touch, leaving them helpless.
  • Addictive Charm: Allure becomes intoxicating, leaving victims craving the Demoness and easy to control.
  • Reflection Curse: Curses a target through their full-body reflection in a mirror.

Sequence 5: Affliction

  • Disease: Releases airborne pathogens that infect people nearby and make them fall ill with unnatural speed.
  • Mirror Curses: Traps foes in mirror illusions and burns any body part a mirror reflects.
  • Active Charm: Charms someone deliberately with a single glance, gesture or word.
  • Hair Weapon: Hair hardens into piercing strands that can inject Black Flames into a victim.
  • Living Threads: Threads become extensions of the body, precise enough to handle small objects.

Sequence 4: Despair

  • Plague: Spreads deadly, specialized plagues up to three kilometers away, filling whole districts with sickness and despair.
  • Petrification: Hair or threads that touch someone stiffen that body part and turn it to stone.
  • Overwhelming Charm: Makes even mid-Sequence Beyonders fall in love and grow addicted within moments.
  • Mirror Maze: Builds mirror mazes, projects itself through mirrors across a city and locates mirrors kilometers away.
  • Gorgon Form: Can take an incomplete Gorgon form whose sight fills onlookers with pleasure and pain.

Sequence 3: Unaging

  • Mirror Self: Merges with a mirror double, freezing their age and making true death almost impossible.
  • Concealed Resurrection: Hides a shielded mirror double to revive in if the main body is destroyed.
  • Spreading Stone: Anything touching infected flesh or spider silk begins turning to stone.
  • Mind-Halting Charm: Charm can freeze a target's thoughts and, given time, make even Angels fall in love.
  • Mirror World Travel: Moves through the Mirror World via eyes and reflections, controlling mirrors across a city.
  • Gorgon Form: Takes a complete Gorgon form with serpent hair that petrifies whatever it looks at.

Sequence 2: Catastrophe

  • Catastrophe: Triggers collapse in people, places and systems, from natural disasters to the breakdown of Beyonder powers.
  • Feared Name: Anyone who speaks the Demoness's name is gripped by deep fear.
  • Curse of Destiny: Cursed objects or words bring a chain of misfortunes to anyone who touches or hears them.
  • Devouring Plague: Pathogens grow strong enough to eat away even complete Mythical Creatures.
  • Many Mirror Selves: Can split off several mirror doubles at once, starting with five.

Sequence 1: Apocalypse

  • Apocalypse: Drives a person, place or system into total contradiction and collapse, even ending a target's destiny.
  • Doomsday Calamities: Unleashes disasters on a scale able to devastate whole regions.

Sequence 0: Primordial Demoness

  • Chaos and Ruin: Returns things to primordial disorder, dissolving targets into swirling, unrealized possibilities.
  • Universal Charm: Charms everything, even abstract concepts, until rules turn against themselves.
  • Mirror Dominion: Controls the entire Mirror World and can appear anywhere within it.
  • Femininity: Holds authority over female forces and can turn beings or objects female.
  • Primordial Plague: Creates countless unseen pathogens that, given time, can infect even True Gods.

Above the Sequence: Calamity of Destruction

A Great Old One holds City of Calamity and the Uniqueness of every pathway it gathers.

Symbolism
Destruction, disaster and catastrophe.
  • Origins of Disaster: Is the source of disasters and calls catastrophes down on cities and nations.
  • Calamity of Destruction: Destroys on the scale of a natural disaster wherever it turns.
