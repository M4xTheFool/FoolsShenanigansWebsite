A wealthy, battle-hardened Backlund viscount whose stern military bearing softens around his wife and his daughter Viola (Nika).

About

Nika's father in Backlund: a wealthy, battle-hardened noble and secret Sequence 5 Disciplinary Paladin.

Backstory

A veteran noble of the Loen Kingdom who secretly reached Sequence 5: Disciplinary Paladin of the Justiciar pathway. For his war service, he was rewarded with a Sequence 4: Bizarro Sorcerer Beyonder characteristic of the Fool pathway, which he locked in his study vault. On 28 June 1349, while returning from a theatre performance with his family, a Secret Order strike team (a Marionettist, a Magician, and a Clown) raided his manor for the characteristic. Though his guards held the line until the Nighthawks arrived, his 18-year-old daughter Viola was fatally shot in the head by the Clown—allowing Nika to transmigrate into her body and shoot the Clown dead. Afterward, Viscount Marlowe handed the Fool characteristic over to the Church of the Evernight Goddess. He quietly deduced that Nika became a Beyonder after the attack and secretly approves of her path, even as she tries to hide it from him.

Abilities

Sequence 9: Arbiter

  • Authority: Carries a commanding weight that makes people inclined to believe their words and follow their instructions.
  • Combat Training: Gains a sturdier body and solid fighting skills for handling sudden violence.
  • Composure of Order: Stays calm under pressure and instinctively works to uphold the existing order.

Sequence 8: Sheriff

  • Jurisdiction: Claims an area as their territory and senses crime and anything unusual happening inside it.
  • Recognition: After seeing someone in person or in a picture, can mystically sense that person, making fugitives hard to lose.
  • Investigative Instinct: Sharpened intuition and spiritual perception help track suspects and notice clues.
  • Weapon Mastery: Handles revolvers, shotguns and melee weapons with practiced confidence.

Sequence 7: Interrogator

  • Psychic Piercing: Fires spiritual lightning from the eyes that bypasses flesh and wounds the soul within a few meters.
  • Whip of Pain: A thorny current lashes the target's consciousness, wearing down resistance and drawing out the truth.
  • Psychic Lashing: Wraps a weapon in spectral lightning so it can harm spirits and incorporeal beings.
  • Spectral Torture Tools: Summons ghostly instruments that injure the spirit body on contact.
  • Weapons and Demolition: Gains expertise with a wide range of weapons and with explosives.

Sequence 6: Judge

  • Verdict: Speaks rules in Ancient Hermes that everything in the surrounding area must obey.
  • Prohibition: Forbids chosen actions or powers in an area, such as flight, invisibility, teleportation or entering the spirit world.
  • Imprisonment and Exile: Verdicts that pin a target in place, seal off an area, or banish a foe from it.
  • Flogging and Death: Verdicts of direct punishment, from painful spiritual lashes to an outright death sentence against weaker foes.
  • Judicial Presence: An unsettling aura makes people avoid their gaze, and their jurisdiction can grow toward city size.

Sequence 5: Disciplinary Paladin

  • Punishment: Marks a person or thing as an offender, gaining strength and precise, hard-to-dodge strikes against it.
  • Overawing Authority: An aura of dread slows nearby foes and pressures them to bow their heads and obey.
  • Layered Prohibitions: Stacks several prohibitions on one area or target at the same time.
  • Paladin's Discipline: Body, intuition and self-control all strengthen, resisting attempts to pry into their mind.

    After Amanises uses Antigonus to divert the Curtain of Fool characteristics into the Forsaken Land of the Gods, the third transmigrator (Zhou Mingrui / Klein Moretti) perishes upon arrival, triggering the release of the fourth cocoon: 22-year-old Polish stage technician Weronika "Nika" Sparrow. Because she bowed on an empty theatre stage at the end of her luck ritual on Earth, the unsealed 10th Sefirah—the Everstage—claims her before the Celestial Worthy's net can bind her to the Fool pathway. She awakens in Backlund in the body of 18-year-old Lady Viola Veronica Marlowe seconds after a Secret Order strike team (a Marionettist, a Magician, and a Clown) raids Viscount Marlowe's manor for a Sequence 4 Bizarro Sorcerer characteristic. Waking in a pool of blood with her fatal head wound healing and a silver-white silk streak in her red hair, Nika grabs a revolver and shoots the Secret Order Clown dead as the Nighthawks arrive. A few nights later, she repeats the ritual in an empty theatre, bows to the dark seats, hears a single clap, and steps onto the Everstage for the first time as the Prompter—recruiting Audrey Hall and Alger Wilson (Odysseus) into the Troupe
