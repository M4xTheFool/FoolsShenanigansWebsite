A tall figure radiating boundless light and majesty, wearing a simple white robe with a silver cross draped over his chest.

About

The Pre-Epoch researcher who walked out of the Chaos Sea at the end of the Second Epoch, slew the Ancient Gods, and ruled the Third Epoch.

Backstory

A modern human from Chernobyl who fell into the Chaos Sea during the cataclysm and awoke in the Second Epoch with the Error Uniqueness and multiple God Almighty authorities. In 1730 of the Second Epoch, he conspired with Amanises and Lilith to bring down Flegrea and assist Amanises in her apotheosis to Sequence 0: Darkness. During his reign in the Third Epoch, he collaborated with Max (Eight), helping him reach Sequence 2: Weather Warlock. When the Primordial God Almighty's will began awakening within him, he established the Rose Redemption with Amanises to orchestrate his own assassination, splitting into the True Creator and Grisha Adam.

Uniqueness

Has absorbed the Uniqueness of the Bard pathway.

A simple silver cross draped over his chest and a blinding golden halo behind his head, accompanied by a crystal monocle (Error Uniqueness) that he later stripped off to give birth to Amon.

Abilities

Sequence 9: Bard

  • Inspiring Song: Sings hymns that boost allies' courage, strength and recovery.
  • Fearless: Resists fear and helps drive it out of others.
  • Hardy Body: Gains a healthier, sturdier body.

Sequence 8: Light Suppliant

  • Sunshine: Calls down scorching sunlight that burns undead and spirits and dazzles foes.
  • Daytime: Floods an area with light as if it were noon.
  • Blessing: Shields allies against fear, cold, darkness and deathly influence.
  • Sun Eyes: Sees in the dark and senses undead and evil beings nearby.
  • Holy Water: Prepares holy water and performs rituals that call stronger light.

Sequence 7: Solar High Priest

  • Holy Light Summoning: Calls down a column of purifying light on evil.
  • Fire of Light: Conjures dense golden flames that burn away corruption.
  • Cleave of Purification: Blesses weapons and bullets with purifying force.
  • Sun Halo: Radiates a halo that steadies courage and cleanses evil influence.
  • Holy Oath: Swears an oath that boosts its body and holy damage.
  • Horror Immunity: Shrugs off supernatural fear.

Sequence 6: Notary

  • Amplification: Notarizes a power or effect as valid, making it stronger.
  • Nullification: Declares an effect invalid, weakening or dispersing it.
  • Authentication: Judges whether a statement, identity, formula or contract is genuine.
  • Binding Contract: Witnesses agreements so that breaking them brings holy punishment.

Sequence 5: Priest of Light

  • Light of Holiness: Calls down a blazing pillar of light that can vaporize targets.
  • Purification Halo: Fills an area with golden radiance that strengthens allies and destroys impure creatures.
  • Bane of the Unclean: Its light and flame hit undead and corrupted beings especially hard.

Sequence 4: Unshadowed

  • Sunbird Body: Becomes a demigod with an incomplete Sunbird form, far stronger, faster and tougher.
  • Unshadowed Domain: Fills an area with blazing light that reveals hidden things and suppresses darkness.
  • Direct Purification: Removes corruption, parasites, illness and mental influence, and in time can cleanse Beyonder characteristics.
  • Unshadowed Spear: Hurls a spear of concentrated holy light.
  • Flaring Sun: Ignites a fierce miniature sun of searing light.
  • Holy Armor: Wraps itself in protective armor and weapons of light.

Sequence 3: Justice Mentor

  • Personal Justice: Turns its own standard of justice into an order binding it, the divine and the world.
  • Justice Halo: Covers a vast region, empowering acts that fit its justice and weakening those that break it.
  • Judgment of Justice: Focuses that suppression onto a single target.
  • Holy Contract: Forms sacred contracts strong enough to restrain angels.
  • Distant Armor: Extends protective holy armor to allies far away.

Sequence 2: Lightseeker

  • Miniature Sun: Becomes a small sun, pouring out immense heat, light and purification.
  • Light Body: Turns into massless light to move at light speed, scatter to avoid harm and reform.
  • Praise the Sun: Produces absolute purification while strengthening itself.
  • Spear of Light: Seals the surrounding space so enemies cannot escape.
  • Justice Trial: Brutally punishes anyone who violates its core justice.

Sequence 1: White Angel

  • Holy Kingdom: Creates a vast radiant domain where darkness, undead and corruption can barely exist.
  • Pure White Light: Purifies by breaking down anything out of harmony with its holiness.
  • Order and Faith: Draws strength from social order and turns people's awe into faith.
  • Regional Sun: Shines as the sun of a whole region, inspiring and reinforcing followers.

Sequence 0: Sun

  • Sun Incarnate: Can act, see and strike wherever light shines.
  • Eternal Day: Brings endless daylight that exposes concealment and suppresses death and darkness.
  • Divine Contracts: Binds even gods with contracts they cannot easily resist.
  • Inextinguishable Light: Extremely hard to destroy, able to return after seeming annihilation.

Events

  1. 1268, Second Epoch
    Awakening of the Ancient Sun GodAwakening
    Chaos Sea (Eastern Continent)
    With Ancient Sun God (Awoke) and Sasrir (Created)
    Sealed Artifacts: First Blasphemy Slate

    At the start of the Early Era of Fire, the Pre-Epoch researcher walks out of the Chaos Sea clutching a silver cross, holding the Sun and Hanged Man pathways alongside the Error Uniqueness. Witnessing humanity enslaved as cattle and rations by the eight mad Ancient Gods of the Dark Epoch, he begins covertly gathering human followers and plotting the liberation of humanity.

  2. 1342, Second Epoch
    Max joins ASGPact or alliance
    Eastern Continent
    With Max, Ancient Sun God, Amanises, Leodoro, Aucuses, Herabergen, Lilith and Ouroboros

    Max joined the Ancient Sun God and his legion as a commander and general, ranked below only the Ancient Sun God and Medici. He quickly became close friends with Amanises, and advanced to Weather Warlock mainly through her: she provided the characteristic, while the Ancient Sun God gave his approval and a place for the ritual, which Max carried out without outside aid. He also grew close to Ouroboros, the weakest of the Ancient Sun God's circle, whom he respected and trained with. Ouroboros warned him not to advance to Sequence 1 "before the Blood Emperor falls".

  3. 1730, Second Epoch
    Ancient ConspiracyBattle and Death

    Amanises together with Ante, Ancient Sun God and Sanguine Ancestor Lilith orchestrated a play to kill: Flegrea, Mutant King Kvastir and supposedly Sanguine Ancestor Lilith.

  4. 1950, Second Epoch
    Amanises' AdvancementAdvancement
    Concealed World
    With Amanises, Ancient Sun God and Max

    What happened: With the Ancient Sun God providing the mystical equivalent of the Moon supplementary ingredient and Lilith lending the Moon Uniqueness as the target of eternal slumber, Amanises advances to Sequence 0: Darkness (the Evernight Goddess) - Max stood guard and made sure everything went smoothly between everyone involved . Together, Amanises (using Concealment) and the Ancient Sun God (using the Error Uniqueness to steal fate) help Lilith replace the fallen Giant Queen Omebella. Meanwhile, the Ancient Sun God manipulates the First Blasphemy Slate to surface, leaking part of the 22 Beyonder pathway formulas to the world and sparking the 230-year "War of the Betrayers" between Giants and Elves.

  5. 2561, Second Epoch
    Twilight of the Ancient GodsBattle and Advancement
    Eastern Continent (Giant King's Court)
    With Ancient Sun God (Attacker), Lilith, Amanises, Herabergen (Surrendered), Max (Witness and support), Aurmir (Falls), Soniathrym (Falls), Leodoro, Aucuses and Salinger

    Following the earlier slayings of Phoenix Ancestor Gregrace and Dragon of Imagination Ankewelt (during which Dragon of Wisdom Herabergen surrendered), the Ancient Sun God slays Elf King Soniathrym and storms the Giant King's Court. Betrayed from within by Badheil and Lilith (masquerading as Giant Queen Omebella), Giant King Aurmir falls. Reclaiming the Sun, Visionary, Hanged Man, Tyrant, and White Tower authorities alongside the Chaos Sea, the Ancient Sun God declares "Let there be light!", ending the 2,561-year Dark Epoch and ushering in the Third Epoch.

  6. 200, Third Epoch
    Birth of Adam and AmonCreation
    Eastern Continent (Kingdom of Silver)
    With Ancient Sun God, Adam, Amon and Medici (Took care of Amon)

    To counteract the Primordial God Almighty's awakening will within him, the Ancient Sun God separates part of his persona and two Sequence 1 Author characteristics with the Visionary Uniqueness to create his eldest son, Angel of Imagination Adam. He also strips off the Error Uniqueness and one Sequence 1 Worm of Time characteristic to give birth to his younger son, Angel of Time Amon (whom War Angel Medici notoriously babysits, burning a lock of the infant Amon's hair). Together with Sasrir, Leodero, Aucuses, Herabergen, Ouroboros, and Medici, they form the Eight Kings of Angels. Max befriended Adam over the following years, and made the acquaintance of Amon, who disliked him but teased him and acted friendly around him. Max saw through the act.

  7. 1086, Third Epoch
    The Fall of Ancient Sun GodBattle and Death
    Eastern Continent (Becomes the Forsaken Land of the Gods)
    With Max (Witness), Amanises (Rose Redemption), Herabergen (Rose Redemption), Leodoro (Rose Redemption), Aucuses (Rose Redemption), Ancient Sun God (Betrayed, dies) and Adam (Witness)

    During the Rose Redemption's attack on the Ancient Sun God inside the Divine Kingdom, Leodero, Aucuses, and Herabergen betray the plan! Instead of allowing the Ancient Sun God to resurrect cleanly in Adam, the three Kings of Angels fall upon his corpse and devour his Tyrant, Sun, and White Tower Uniquenesses and Sequence 1 characteristics to ascend to Sequence 0 on the spot. Enraged by the betrayal, the Ancient Sun God's resentment, humanity, and remaining Hanged Man characteristics fuse into the mad True Creator, ruining Adam's immediate resurrection plan (forcing Adam to remain a King of Angels until the Fifth Epoch). The resulting divine cataclysm shatters the Eastern Continent into the Forsaken Land of the Gods, rips open the Abyss and the Sea of Ruins, and leaves the Second Blasphemy Slate o condense from the Ancient Sun God's fallen flesh and blood. Sasrir falls into eternal slumber in the Giant King's Court holding the First Blasphemy Slate
