Sealed Artifact

The King's Will

Sealed Artifact A-027, Monster (Wheel of Fortune) pathway, Sequence 2 level, mixed with the Seer pathway

Mixed characteristics

Characteristics

  • Monster (Wheel of Fortune) pathway, Sequence 2 (its main one)
  • Seer (The Fool) pathway, Sequence 2

Appearance

A crown of liquid silver that slowly circles its wearer's brow. Look at it long enough and the band becomes a serpent swallowing its own tail. Fine streams of quicksilver cross the band from top to bottom: they rise above the head like the points of a crown and fall in front of the face, never touching the skin. The serpent's scales reflect countless tiny points of light, like stars seen through water, and each one is a miracle. When the crown is in use, the streams quicken and pour down in front of the wearer's eyes.

Ability

The wearer sees about 30 seconds into the future, across roughly a battlefield's width; both can be amplified by the wearer's own authorities and abilities. From the futures it shows, the wearer chooses the one he favours.

Passive: raises the wearer's luck and lowers the luck of the enemies he chooses.

Active: amplifies that luck and misfortune, and the power of miracles helps the chosen future come about more easily.

Limits: a True God can contest the chosen future, if they know what to contest and push hard enough.

Drawback

Drawbacks: a small spirituality drain and a heavy mental load. When people the wearer loves are involved, it shows him their worst outcomes first.

Notes

Reforged by Max and Ouroboros after they met post Forsaken Land arc. The characteristic of Miracle Invoker was Nikas.
