An ancient deity of breathtaking beauty and maternal warmth who secretly masqueraded as the resurrected Giant Queen Omebella for three epochs while remaining the true Ancestor of the Sanguine.

About

The Second Epoch Sanguine Ancestor who feigned her death in 1730, lent the Moon Uniqueness to Amanises, and became the Earth Mother.

Backstory

Originally the Ancient God of the Sanguine holding Sequence 0: Moon in the Second Epoch, Lilith suffered heavy corruption from the Mother Goddess of Depravity. In 1730 of the Second Epoch, she conspired with Amanises and the Ancient Sun God, feigning her death in a battle that killed Flegrea and Kvastir. She handed her Moon Uniqueness to Amanises—who put it into eternal slumber to complete the Sequence 0: Darkness ritual—and with the help of Amanises' Concealment and the Ancient Sun God's Error authority, stole the identity and fate of the fallen Giant Queen Omebella, switching to Sequence 0: Mother as the Earth Mother.

Abilities

Sequence 9: Apothecary (Apothecary pathway)

  • Medicine Concoction: Brews herbal remedies and potions that cure most illnesses and injuries, short of terminal ones.
  • Poison Craft: Prepares poisons from herbs and has modest resistance to poisons and toxins.
  • Spirit Vision: Sees a person's overall health and well-being at a glance.

Sequence 8: Beast Tamer (Apothecary pathway)

  • Taming: Tames, domesticates and commands animals, even Beyonder creatures.
  • Animal Sense: Talks with animals, shares their senses and reads their emotions and intentions.

Sequence 7: Vampire (Apothecary pathway)

  • Rapid Regeneration: Becomes a vampire whose wounds close at unnatural speed, making it very hard to kill.
  • Wings of Darkness: Sprouts shadowy bat wings for swift flight, or bursts them into a swarm of attacking bats.
  • Blood Drain: Drinks a victim's blood to sap their life and restore its own strength.
  • Claws of Corrosion: Extends sharp, corrosive nails that cut steel and tear through scales or hide.
  • Abyss Shackles: Condenses darkness into chains that bind and restrain enemies.
  • The Embrace: Converts humans into vampires or into obedient blood servants.

Sequence 6: Potions Professor (Apothecary pathway)

  • Specialty Potions: Brews potions with Beyonder effects, such as invisibility, fire breathing, dreamless sleep or shadow cloaking.
  • Material Discernment: Instantly judges the properties of spiritual ingredients and how to combine them.
  • Sun Water: Prepares a holy liquid that badly harms undead and vampires.
  • Cure for the Incurable: Medicines can now treat even terminal illnesses.
  • Physical Enhancement: Gains greater speed, agility and strength.

Sequence 5: Scarlet Scholar (Apothecary pathway)

  • Lunar Battlefield: Shapes the area with moonlight, conjuring a full-moon field or a moonless dark that favors the Scholar.
  • Moonlight Flash: Teleports short distances whenever moonlight is present.
  • Moonlight Body: Turns into condensed moonlight that reforms after shattering unless the heart is struck.

Sequence 4: Shaman King (Apothecary pathway)

  • Instant Rituals: Performs rituals instantly by drawing on nature, including raising corpses as undead servants.
  • Moon Paper Figurine: Swaps places with a paper figure made of moonlight that takes the hit instead.
  • Gaze of Darkness: Curses a target through a linked eyeball, restraining or gravely wounding them via the eye.
  • Bat Swarm Body: Splits into a swarm of bats and reforms unless every single bat is destroyed.

Sequence 3: High Summoner (Apothecary pathway)

  • Door of Summoning: Opens an illusory door to call powerful creatures from the Spirit World and other realms.
  • Mythical Creature Form: Takes a fuller mythical form and can hear prayers offered within a certain range.

Sequence 2: Life-Giver (Apothecary pathway)

  • Life Creation: Brings entirely new living beings into existence.
  • Proliferation: Spurs living things around it to multiply and flourish.

Sequence 1: Beauty Goddess (Apothecary pathway)

  • Divine Beauty: Possesses a pure, godlike beauty that inspires admiration and a wish to protect her.
  • Charm of Beauty: Turns that beauty into a pull on others' spirituality, the source of her strongest powers.
  • Femininity: Embodies feminine yin energy.

Sequence 0: Mother (Planter pathway)

  • Motherhood: Gives birth to all things and can awaken life even in the inanimate.
  • Earth: As the origin and resting place of all things, rules the cycle of life, death and return.
  • Nature: Has near-indestructible vitality and raises plant barriers able to hold back True Gods.
  • Life and Death: Makes creatures grow and mutate uncontrollably, or drains the life even of gods.
  • Bountiful Earth: Rules a fertile divine kingdom, with lesser authority over harvest, healing and desolation.

    Max joined the Ancient Sun God and his legion as a commander and general, ranked below only the Ancient Sun God and Medici. He also grew close to Ouroboros, the weakest of the Ancient Sun God's circle, whom he respected and trained with. Ouroboros warned him not to advance to Sequence 1 "before the Blood Emperor falls".

Reclaiming the Sun, Visionary, Hanged Man, Tyrant, and White Tower authorities alongside the Chaos Sea, the Ancient Sun God declares "Let there be light!", ending the 2,561-year Dark Epoch and ushering in the Third Epoch.
