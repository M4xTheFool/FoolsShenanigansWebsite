Sealed Artifact

Arrodes

Sealed Artifact A-002, Bard (Sun) pathway, mixed with the Spectator, Sailor, Reader and Secrets Supplicant pathways

Mixed characteristics

Characteristics

  • Bard (Sun) pathway, level unknown (its main one)
  • Spectator (Visionary) pathway, level unknown
  • Sailor (Tyrant) pathway, level unknown
  • Reader (White Tower) pathway, level unknown
  • Secrets Supplicant (Hanged Man) pathway, level unknown

Appearance

A silver mirror with ancient patterns on the back and an eye-like pattern on each side of its face.

Ability

A sentient, talkative magic mirror that answers questions put to it.

Drawback

Every answer has a price: it asks a question back, and anyone who refuses or answers falsely must accept a punishment or answer something embarrassing.

Notes

Formerly Sealed Artifact 2-111, now treated as Grade 1 with its exact grade unknown. In the original novel, Arrodes belonged to the Church of the God of Steam and Machinery. Ikanser Bernard of the Machinery Hivemind carried it on investigations in Backlund, and Archbishop Horamick Haydn came to suspect it was Grade 0. It later attached itself to Klein, whom it called "Great Master" and "Honorable existence above the spirit world". In this story there is no one above the gray fog for it to bow to, and for years it stays with the Machinery Hivemind. The strongest hidden presence it can find instead is the Everstage. It offers itself to the Phantom as "your loyal and humble servant", and by 1360 it hangs in the Prompter's dressing room. It knew there was no Phantom long before the Truth Play. It was on the battlefield on 25 June 1360.
