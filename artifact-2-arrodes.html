Sealed Artifact

Arrodes

Sealed Artifact A-002, Bard (Sun) pathway

Appearance

A silver mirror with ancient patterns on the back and an eye-like pattern on each side of its face.

Ability

A sentient, talkative magic mirror that answers questions put to it.

Drawback

Every answer has a price: it asks a question back, and anyone who refuses or answers falsely must accept a punishment or answer something embarrassing.

Notes

Formerly Sealed Artifact 2-111, now treated as Grade 1 with its exact grade unknown (first appears Ch. 351). Ikanser Bernard held it before Klein; it then served Klein, and it is the reason Dwayne Dantès got a womanizer's reputation.

Held By

  • Klein Moretti (C-001)
