A woman of average height with slightly curly brown hair and pale blue eyes. She dresses plainly and often has a cigarette or a drink close at hand.

About

Lazy bestselling romance novelist and Door-pathway Beyonder, disciple of the Abraham family.

Backstory

Fors Wall trained as a doctor after a hard childhood: her father, a low-ranking officer, abandoned the family, and her mother died while Fors was still in grammar school. Working at the Yosifov Clinic, she cared for Mrs. Aulisa, a widow from a branch of the Abraham family, who left her savings, books and the formula for a Door pathway travel bracelet. That inheritance made her a Door pathway Beyonder and gave her room to become a bestselling romance novelist. She later became a disciple of Dorian Gray Abraham. When the full-moon ravings of Mr. Door nearly drove her mad, she prayed with the Phantom's honorific, which she had learned from Nika, and in 1350 she found the Everstage on her own. Nika pulled her in as Lucy, and on the stage the cries fell silent.

When Max offered her, Xio and Audrey a trip into the Forsaken Land, only Fors went, as a Sequence 5 Traveler. Nobody can leave the land the way they came in, so she stayed until the road out opened. Max helped her advance there and the two grew close. Before she left, she watched the leaving fight beside Nika to record its powers, and she came out a Sequence 4 Secrets Sorcerer in about 1353. They became lovers later. Bethel Abraham, the Mr. Door of her full moons, died in about 1358. Amon took his Sequence 1 and the Door Uniqueness and left the rest to the Abraham family, which gives Fors a chance to climb higher. Her closest friend and roommate is Xio Derecha.

    After Amanises uses Antigonus to divert the Curtain of Fool characteristics into the Forsaken Land of the Gods, the third transmigrator (Zhou Mingrui / Klein Moretti) perishes upon arrival, triggering the release of the fourth cocoon: 22-year-old Polish stage technician Weronika "Nika" Sparrow. Because she bowed on an empty theatre stage at the end of her luck ritual on Earth, the unsealed 10th Sefirah—the Everstage—claims her before the Celestial Worthy's net can bind her to the Fool pathway. She awakens in Backlund in the body of 18-year-old Lady Viola Veronica Marlowe seconds after a Secret Order strike team (a Marionettist, a Magician, and a Clown) raids Viscount Marlowe's manor for a Sequence 4 Bizarro Sorcerer characteristic. Waking in a pool of blood with her fatal head wound healing and a silver-white silk streak in her red hair, Nika grabs a revolver and shoots the Secret Order Clown dead as the Nighthawks arrive. A few nights later, she repeats the ritual in an empty theatre, bows to the dark seats, hears a single clap, and steps onto the Everstage for the first time as the Prompter—recruiting Audrey Hall and Alger Wilson (Odysseus) into the Troupe
