Sequences

Sequence 9: Savant

  • Perfect Recall: Remembers everything they have read, seen or experienced.
  • Engineering Mastery: Quickly grasps machines, firearms and lab equipment, repairing and improving them beyond normal standards.
  • Rapid Learning: Absorbs mathematics, physics, chemistry and technical crafts at remarkable speed.

Sequence 8: Archaeologist

  • Historical Insight: Reads an object's age, origin, purpose and dangers from its construction, wear, symbols and spiritual traces.
  • Ruin Lore: Knows ancient languages, lost cultures and the taboos and traps hidden in ruins.
  • Field Hardiness: Gains a sturdy body and survival skills for long expeditions in the wild.

Sequence 7: Appraiser

  • Appraisal: Quickly works out the properties, activation and drawbacks of most mystical items without divination.
  • Authentication: Spots forgeries, hidden damage, contamination and an item's true worth.
  • Safe Handling: Uses unfamiliar sealed artifacts while sidestepping much of their negative side effects.

Sequence 6: Artisan

  • Crafting: Builds mystical items and Beyonder weapons from characteristics, spiritual materials, symbols and machinery.
  • Artifact Repair: Mends damaged mystical items, changes how they activate and reduces some of their drawbacks.
  • Advanced Machinery: Designs mechanical devices well beyond the technology of the era.

Sequence 5: Astronomer

  • Astronomy: Understands celestial phenomena well enough to draw power and guidance from the stars.
  • False Starry Sky: Projects a fake sky of constellations that misleads astrology and other divination.
  • Star of Curses: Condenses starlight into rays that weaken, mutate or injure a target.
  • Star Blessing: Uses absorbed starlight to change gravity for a chosen target or area.
  • Celestial Calculation: Precisely computes trajectories, distances and timing for navigation and targeting.

Sequence 4: Alchemist

  • Transmutation: Reshapes and converts materials, alloys and energy with extreme precision.
  • Life Alchemy: Creates artificial life such as golems, mechanical creatures and altered plants, and can give objects limited intelligence.
  • Elixirs: Brews alchemical concoctions that strengthen allies or weaken enemies.

Sequence 3: Arcane Scholar

  • Body of Knowledge: Transforms into a form made of knowledge for anywhere from minutes to days.
  • Environment Analysis: Extracts precise information from the surroundings to reconstruct what happened there.
  • Life Absorption: Drains vitality from living things across a vast radius.
  • Disassembly Ray: Fires a beam of light that breaks down the structure of most objects.
  • Arcane Technology: Translates observed powers into reproducible machines, rituals and artifacts.

Sequence 2: Knowledge Magister

  • Teaching: Pours knowledge and skills into others, or strips learned skills away from them.
  • Analysis: Breaks any power, object or phenomenon down to its working principles and designs counters to it.
  • Enlightenment: Sparks sudden understanding in a target, granting temporary mastery of a field.
  • Rapid Creation: Turns complex designs into working creations far faster and awakens intelligence in machines.

Sequence 1: Illuminator

  • Civilization: Holds authority over civilization, seeing a society's potential and the inventions it needs next.
  • Illumination: Inspires discoveries and speeds education so communities leap centuries ahead.
  • Collective Mastery: Temporarily grants whole communities the knowledge and coordination for immense projects.

Sequence 0: Paragon

  • Divine Creation: Creates artifacts, machines, cities and artificial beings that would otherwise take whole civilizations.
  • Authority of Knowledge: Commands practical knowledge and learning, teaching or dissecting anything down to its principles.
  • Technology Command: Awakens, suppresses, repairs, transforms or absorbs machinery and crafted objects.
  • Progress: Accelerates invention and turns industry, transport and institutions into extensions of their will.

Above the Sequence: Demon of Knowledge

A Great Old One holds Knowledge Moor and the Uniqueness of every pathway it gathers.

Symbolism
Knowledge, and the madness that comes with knowing too much.
  • Demon of Knowledge: Knows, and grants, any knowledge, always at a price.
  • Arcana of Madness: Knowledge taken from it drives the one who takes it toward madness.
