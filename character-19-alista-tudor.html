From the Lawyer pathway (Black Emperor) to the Hunter pathway (Red Priest). Not neighbours: a risky switch that tends to cost sanity.

Each Sequence gives the abilities of the pathway it was taken from. Stat bonuses and the speed grade follow the pathway they are on now, the Hunter pathway.

Abilities

Sequence 9: Lawyer (Lawyer pathway)

  • Eloquence: Argues with persuasive force and sharp logic, quietly steering how listeners judge a case.
  • Exploit Loopholes: Spots gaps in rules, contracts and procedures, and weak points in an opponent's position, then turns them to advantage.
  • Subtle Influence: Their reasoning sways people almost unnoticed, slipping past spiritual intuition and even working when they do not intend it.

Sequence 8: Barbarian (Lawyer pathway)

  • Barbarian Body: Strength, toughness and stamina rise far beyond human limits, making them a ferocious close-quarters fighter.
  • Iron Will: Shrugs off persuasion, intimidation and other mental influence that would sway ordinary people.

Sequence 7: Briber (Lawyer pathway)

  • Bribery: Handing over any real or symbolic payment, even a tossed coin the target refuses, opens a channel for supernatural influence.
  • Weaken: A bribe saps the target's strength, lowering their attack, defense and resistance.
  • Charm: A bribe fills the target with goodwill toward the Briber and softens their hostility.
  • Arrogance: A bribe inflates the target's pride so they underestimate danger and misjudge the fight.
  • Bond: A bribe ties a mystical thread between Briber and target that later powers can build on.

Sequence 6: Baron of Corruption (Lawyer pathway)

  • Corruption: Stirs greed and dark desires in everyone within about ten meters, pushing them toward reckless, immoral choices.
  • Distortion: Twists the meaning of words, actions and intentions, so a strike becomes a miss or shutting a door seals a room.
  • Redirection: Bends an attack's path or a supernatural effect's target toward a harmless or favorable result.
  • Corrupt Strength: Physical power surges again, with blows that hit like cannon shells.

Sequence 5: Mentor of Disorder (Lawyer pathway)

  • Disorder: Throws a target or area into chaos, so rules fail, attacks miss, distances mislead and foes choose badly.
  • Imperious Presence: Radiates a majesty that makes weaker people want to bow and carry out their orders.
  • Shared Harm: Distortion now spreads curses, illnesses and injuries along mystical links, forcing connected targets to share the damage.
  • Slip Free: Combines disorder and distortion to escape another's control or a losing position.

Sequence 4: Earl of the Fallen (Lawyer pathway)

  • Exploit: Stretches or cuts short an existing state, such as prolonging a jump into flight or keeping an opening from closing.
  • Magnify: Hugely amplifies one action, wound or effect, turning a scratch fatal or a gust into a gale.
  • Bestowment: Curses a target with money obsession, greedy impatience, lost fighting spirit or sluggish limpness.
  • Rule Bending: Uses Exploit, Magnify and Distortion together to claim an area and rewrite its rules in their favor.

Sequence 3: Frenzied Mage (Lawyer pathway)

  • Superiority: Converts legitimate rank, wealth and command into crushing pressure; the voice cows lesser beings and the body can grow giant.
  • Frenzy: Makes powers mutate unpredictably, usually improving the Mage's own while warping enemies' abilities into weak or absurd forms.
  • Frenzied Distortion: Bends projectiles mid-flight and hijacks a target's intent to move so they cannot stop themselves.

Sequence 2: Duke of Entropy (Lawyer pathway)

  • Entropy: The longer a fight lasts, the more random a foe's powers, thoughts and surroundings become, until they collapse entirely.
  • Angelic Exploit: Pushes states, loopholes and distances to impossible extremes, such as one leap carrying them to the crimson moon.
  • Rule Distortion: Twists rules set by other authorities and bends them to the Duke's will.

Sequence 1: Prince of Abolition (Lawyer pathway)

  • Redefinition: Redefines what counts as a bribe, an identity, an authority or the original body, then bends order around that definition.
  • Abolition: Overturns or voids chosen laws and orders, replacing them through prepared definitions and superior standing.
  • Regional Isolation: Seals off a prepared region by redefining what counts as inside, outside, law and protection.

Sequence 0: Red Priest (Hunter pathway)

  • Authority of War: Embodies war and the hunt, spreading conflict and destructive urges and ruling every soldier who fights.
  • Destruction: Destroys physical and even conceptual things, able to collapse space-time with a clenched fist.
  • Divine Conquest: Conquers anything, even abstract ideas, and forms a divine kingdom from conquered land.
  • Masculinity: Holds authority over male forces and can turn beings or objects male.
