Unshadowed Crucifix

Sealed Artifact A-008, Bard (Sun) pathway, Sequence 4 level

Appearance

A copper-green bronze cross wrapped in thorns.

Ability

Grants power comparable to a Sequence 4 Unshadowed: it purifies corruption and can drive Beyonder characteristics out.

Drawback

It can't be used together with other mystical items, slowly drags the user's Sequence down (angels excepted) and gradually turns the user into a devout Sun believer.

Notes

Grade 1 relic of the ancient Sun god (first appears Ch. 1021). Fors and Xio handed it over as a sacrifice; Derrick's Sequence 4 advancement (Ch. 1227) drew on it, and the wiki lists it as destroyed. No holder at the snapshot.
