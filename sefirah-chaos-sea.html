A sea of chaos left by the Original Creator, the source of matter and energy. It is the only Sefirah that merges the real and the illusory and can be entered from the physical world. Its Great Old One, God Almighty, is one of the Three Pillars (Ch. 1347). With Sefirah Castle it is one of the two Sefirot that were never sealed away (Ch. 1369).

Above the Sequence

Symbolism
Creation, the source of matter and energy, and the place where the real and the illusory merge.
  • Omnipotent and Omniscient: Sees and can do almost anything within the Chaos Sea's reach, as the full title says.
  • Source of Matter and Energy: Draws matter and energy out of the Chaos Sea to make whatever it needs.
  • Real and Illusory: Merges the real and the imagined, so what it pictures can become real.

Known Holders

  • The Ancient Sun God, who inherited it (the wiki cites about Ch. 1033)
  • Sasrir, made from part of the Ancient Sun God's personality to control it, and corrupted by it (wiki)
  • Adam, who with the True Creator drew on it to become Half a Great Old One for a while (Ch. 1376)

Held By

  • Adam (C-012), Sequence 0
  • Ancient Sun God (C-021), Above the Sequence

Notes

Full title on the Second Blasphemy Slate: God Almighty, Omnipotent and Omniscient (Ch. 1345). Sequel: in Circle of Inevitability, Grisha Adam accommodates it and merges with the Primordial God Almighty. The symbolism and Great Old One abilities are an interpretation of the full title and the description; the novel never lists them.
