Sealed Artifact 2-049 (Antigonus Family Puppet)

Sealed Artifact A-006, Seer (The Fool) pathway, Sequence 5 level

Appearance

A puppet wrapped in oil-stained brown cloth, its face painted like a red-and-yellow clown.

Ability

Lets its user control others like puppets, comparable to a Sequence 5 Marionettist.

Drawback

Its effect also turns back on the user.

Notes

Grade 2 Sealed Artifact (first appears Ch. 70), tied to the Antigonus family and held by the Church of the Evernight Goddess.
