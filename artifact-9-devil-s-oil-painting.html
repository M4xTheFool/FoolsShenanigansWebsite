Sealed Artifact

Devil's Oil Painting

Sealed Artifact A-009, Apprentice (Door) pathway, Sequence 4 level

Appearance

An oil painting of abstract, incomprehensible shapes.

Ability

Banishes and seals powerful beings like a Sequence 4 Secrets Sorcerer, strongly enough to imprison demigod-level creatures.

Drawback

Staring at it causes dizziness and nausea, and over time it wears down the viewer's mind.

Notes

Grade 1, from the Abraham family (first appears Ch. 1293). Fors used it during her advancement to Secrets Sorcerer; listed under her, though the wiki doesn't say whether she kept it.

Held By

  • Fors Wall (C-005)
