Master Key

Sealed Artifact A-010, Apprentice (Door) pathway, Sequence 9 level

Appearance

An old key.

Ability

Opens locks and lets its holder pass through obstacles, like a Sequence 9 Apprentice.

Drawback

Its curse leaves the user lost and unable to find their way.

Notes

Grade 3 (first appears Ch. 309). Emlyn White took it from a hospital thief, and its curse led to his capture by Father Utravsky. It was later destroyed, so no holder at the snapshot.
