General of the Pupil-less Eye

Sealed Artifact A-015, Prisoner (Chained) pathway, Sequence 4 level

Appearance

An exquisitely crafted male puppet. It is only the size of a palm and wearing a dark red gold-encrusted dress. Only two black holes left on its eye sockets.

Ability

This item could could possess a target and control every non-living thing within a certain radius according to the holder's thought. It also had some level of control over mystical items that no one possessed or had spirituality injected into.

Drawback

It has a living characteristic. User needs to feed it with their own flesh or it would grow its eyes back gradually. Once its eyes are intact and left unsealed, it will turn into an evil spirit that hates all human beings and would chase their owner crazily.
