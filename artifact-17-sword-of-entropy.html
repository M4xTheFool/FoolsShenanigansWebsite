Sword of Entropy

Sealed Artifact A-017, Lawyer (Black Emperor) pathway, Sequence 2 level

Appearance

A seemingly shattered sword that holds its shape. Its made of black steel with a fancy handguard.

Ability

It disorders anything it hits - beyonders, clothes, ground. It can exploit any weakness or smallest crack in the blade that hits against it to shatter it instantly.

Drawback

There are spikes on the grip that continously take blood from the holder at a fast rate until they die as long as they're held.

Notes

When sheathed in the Sheathe of Order, all of the drawbacks of both items cancel out, as long as they're withing 10 meters of each other and the sword is sheathed at least once for 5 minutes every 6 hours.
