A young gentleman with neat black hair, dark brown eyes and gold-rimmed glasses. He looks colder and harder than Klein's other faces and gives off the gloom of a seasoned, ruthless adventurer.

About

Klein Moretti's persona: a silent, reckless pirate-hunting adventurer who sits in the club as The World.

Backstory

Gehrman Sparrow is not a separate person: he is one of Klein Moretti's personas, built with Faceless shapeshifting and played with complete commitment. In the Tarot Club he sits as The World and is presented to the others as a fervent follower of The Fool, so the members treat him as one of their own. Outside the club he is a taciturn, reckless adventurer who started out hunting pirates and was drawn into more and more demigod-level Beyonder incidents. His wanted bounty stood at 50,000 pounds after the Bayam incident and later climbed to 90,000 pounds. For a time he traveled aboard Cattleya's flagship Future. Because Gehrman is Klein, this sheet uses Klein's pathway and Sequence at the snapshot.

Abilities

Sequence 9: Seer

  • Spirit Vision: Sees auras, emotions, health and spirits that ordinary eyes miss.
  • Divination: Answers questions through dowsing rods, pendulums, dreams, mirrors, cards and similar methods.
  • Danger Intuition: Feels a vague warning when threats or bad omens are drawing near.
  • Mystic Aptitude: Gains a sharper memory, stronger spirituality and a natural knack for rituals.

Sequence 8: Clown

  • Acrobatics: Gains superb balance, agility and body control for leaps, flips and dodges.
  • Poker Face: Keeps expressions and bodily reactions calm and unreadable, even under pressure.
  • Combat Intuition: Senses an incoming attack a split second before it lands.
  • Paper Blades: Hardens playing cards or paper so they can be thrown or swung like knives.

Sequence 7: Magician

  • Paper Figurine Substitute: Swaps places with a paper figure to escape a lethal blow or blunt a curse.
  • Flaming Jump: Steps into one fire and out of another flame nearby.
  • Air Bullet: Snaps a finger to fire a compressed burst of air with gun-like force.
  • Illusions: Creates convincing false sights and sounds to mislead onlookers.
  • Escape Artistry: Softens bones and joints to slip out of ropes, cuffs and tight spots.
  • Damage Transfer: Shifts an injury away from vital organs to a less dangerous place.

Sequence 6: Faceless

  • Shapeshifting: Reshapes face, body, voice and scent to become someone else, within limits of size.
  • Perfect Mimicry: Copies a person's looks, mannerisms and habits convincingly after studying them.
  • Face Memory: Remembers faces precisely and recognizes familiar people through their disguises.

Sequence 5: Marionettist

  • Spirit Body Threads: Sees and grasps the invisible threads linking every being to its spirit body.
  • Marionette Control: Slows a victim through their threads until they become an obedient marionette that keeps its powers.
  • Marionette Swap: Instantly trades places with one of its marionettes within range.
  • Remote Bodies: Acts, speaks and fights through its marionettes as if they were extra bodies.

Sequence 4: Bizarro Sorcerer

  • Bizarro Body: Becomes a demigod with an eerie, partly inhuman body of spirit worms that is very hard to kill.
  • Spirit World Shortcut: Briefly passes through the Spirit World to cover distance, and flame jumps reach much farther.
  • Marionette Legion: Commands dozens of marionettes and can use its own powers through worms planted in them.
  • Information Concealment: Hides facts about itself from divination and prying senses.
  • Beast Forms: Transforms into animals.
  • Wound Transfer: Moves its injuries or illnesses onto paper figures.

Sequence 3: Scholar of Yore

  • Historical Projection: Summons temporary projections of people or objects it knows out of history.
  • Past Self: Borrows the state or strength of an earlier version of itself.
  • Historical Void: Enters the fog of history and hides inside gaps in the past.

Sequence 2: Miracle Invoker

  • Wish Granting: Makes wishes come true within limits, with bigger miracles needing many gathered wishes.
  • Miracle Feats: Restores what was lost, moves things, reshapes an area, extends life or briefly suppresses powers.
  • Angelic Form: Completes its mythical creature form and becomes an angel.

Sequence 1: Attendant of Mysteries

  • Grafting: Links things that have no real connection, joining places, effects, definitions or concepts.
  • Realm of Mysteries: Wraps an area in mystery, hiding it and cutting it off from the outside.
  • Thread Restoration: Gives spirit body threads back to things that once had them, and makes marionettes rapidly.
  • Direct Miracles: Performs miracles directly instead of waiting on accumulated wishes.

Sequence 0: Fool

  • Fooling: Deceives senses, thought, history, fate and even reality, so a false condition can become true.
  • Lord of Mysteries: Holds authority over mysteries, the hidden and the unknown.
  • Perfected Powers: Grafting, miracles, history, marionettes and divination all reach full divine strength.

Above the Sequence: Lord of the Mysteries

Symbolism
Mysteries, the gray fog above reality, the Spirit World, and space and time.
  • Lord of the Mysteries: Holds authority over every mystery, everything hidden and everything unknown.
  • Dominator of the Spirit World: Rules the Spirit World and the spirit bodies that drift through it.
  • King of Space-Time: Commands space and time, as the full title on the Second Blasphemy Slate says.
  • Castle above the Gray Fog: Pulls spirit bodies into Sefirah Castle, hears every prayer addressed to it and hides what happens there from divination.

Details

A thin black glove of human skin with a hidden maw in its palm.

Ability
It devours the souls of Beyonders and keeps them inside, letting the wearer borrow their powers. These match Sequence 7 to 5 Hanged Man pathway abilities, such as driving targets mad, entering dreams and changing appearance.
Drawback
It has to be fed. While sealed it eats a soul each time it is used; otherwise it wants a living person's flesh and soul every other day, and a starving glove will turn on its own wearer.
