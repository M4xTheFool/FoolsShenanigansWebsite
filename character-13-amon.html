A young-looking man with black hair, black eyes, a broad forehead and a thin face, in a classic black robe and a pointed hat (Ch. 1374, 1387). He always wears a crystal monocle, and so do his countless avatars, whether they are postmen, birds or microbes (Ch. 1377).

About

The Angel of Time and God of Mischief: a Sequence 0 Error who also took the Door pathway's Uniqueness, a dual-pathway true god.

Backstory

Amon is a son of the Ancient Sun God, the Angel of Time and one of the eight Kings of Angels, a trickster known as the God of Mischief and the Blasphemer. His father called him the light at the dawn of the apocalypse (Ch. 1063). He spreads through avatars that each believe they are Amon and lodges himself inside others; a parasitic link with Klein Moretti let him read Klein's thoughts (Ch. 1152). He stole the First Blasphemy Slate as Sasrir's shadow palace collapsed (Ch. 1265). In Fall 1352 he completed the Error ritual, replacing someone during their apotheosis: Mr. Door turned into Amon, holding a key of pure starlight (Ch. 1353). He went on to accommodate the Door Uniqueness and its authority, becoming a dual-pathway true god (Ch. 1375, 1377). In the final battle he spread countless monocled avatars, stole moments of lucidity and froze time (Ch. 1377-1385), until, inside Sefirah Castle, a supernova shattered his monocle and destroyed his true body (Ch. 1386-1387). Klein took in his Error and Door Uniquenesses. One avatar that had been sleeping at Adam's place survived, found it could no longer summon a monocle, and left to enter the cosmos.

Abilities

Sequence 9: Marauder

  • Theft: Lifts items from pockets, bags and bodies without the owner noticing.
  • Nimble Hands: Gains fast, precise hands and quick reflexes.
  • Keen Eye: Spots valuables, hiding places and openings for a theft.

Sequence 8: Swindler

  • Deceit: Tells persuasive lies that briefly bend a target's thinking toward believing them.
  • Silver Tongue: Gains eloquence and charm that make people listen and trust.
  • Misdirection: Draws attention away from its hands and its real goals.

Sequence 7: Cryptologist

  • Decryption: Breaks codes, ciphers and secret writing with ease.
  • Pattern Sight: Spots hidden patterns, contradictions and clues that others overlook.
  • Flaw Finding: Perceives weak points in people, defenses, rituals and seals.

Sequence 6: Prometheus

  • Power Theft: Steals a Beyonder ability from a nearby target and can use it for a short time.
  • Borrowed Fire: Turns a stolen power against its owner or others while the theft lasts.

Sequence 5: Dream Stealer

  • Intent Theft: Steals a target's thought or intention, so they forget what they meant to do.
  • Memory Theft: Takes memories from a target, learning what they knew.
  • Dream Theft: Steals into dreams to take secrets and ideas.

Sequence 4: Parasite

  • Parasitism: Lodges inside another being to hide, watch through them or control them from within.
  • Worm Avatars: Splits off small worm-like bodies that parasitize others and act as its avatars.
  • Time Worm Form: Becomes a demigod with an incomplete Worm of Time form that is very hard to wipe out.

Sequence 3: Mentor of Deceit

  • Loophole Exploit: Finds and abuses gaps in rules, contracts, seals and even natural laws.
  • Deceive the Rules: Tricks the world's rules into accepting a false situation as valid.
  • Induced Errors: Nudges enemies and their powers into small but costly mistakes.

Sequence 2: Trojan Horse of Destiny

  • Fate Theft: Steals parts of another's fate, taking their luck, role or future for itself.
  • Fate Parasitism: Hides inside a target's destiny, waiting to seize it at the right moment.
  • Fate Swap: Exchanges destinies between two people, or between itself and another.

Sequence 1: Worm of Time

  • Time Theft: Steals moments of time from others, skipping or erasing what they would have done.
  • Time Parasitism: Lodges itself within time and others' timelines, making it extremely hard to kill.
  • Countless Avatars: Splits into many Worms of Time, each a capable avatar.

Sequence 0: Error

  • Authority of Error: Rules over loopholes, mistakes and deceit, making rules and powers fail.
  • Absolute Theft: Can steal almost anything, from objects and powers to fate, time and concepts.
  • Deceiving the World: Fools reality itself into treating false or impossible situations as real.

Notes

Snapshot: Amon at his peak as a dual-pathway true god (Ch. 1375-1386), before his defeat. At the very end of the first novel his true body is gone, Klein holds the Error and Door Uniquenesses, and only an avatar of unknown Sequence survives (Ch. 1387). Canon: his look (Ch. 1374, 1377, 1387), his titles, the Error ritual (Ch. 1353), the Door Uniqueness (Ch. 1375), dual-pathway true god (Ch. 1377), and the Worm of Time and Key of Stars characteristics Klein takes from him (Ch. 1387), which is why the Door pathway here has one Key of Stars characteristic. A guess: which Door powers the Uniqueness gives him (the novel only says he obtained its authority), his age, alignment and stats (a 27-point buy before pathway bonuses). His height and his birthday, 3 January, come from character profiles quoted online, not from the chapters. No honorific name: the three-line versions on the wiki have no source, and the Ch. 1152 line about 'a trojan horse of fate, the slug of time' describes the Error pathway, not him. Left out: the sequel, Circle of Inevitability, where the surviving avatar replaces Termiboros and later becomes a Sequence 1 Angel of Redemption.
