A towering, heavily muscled youth with brownish-yellow hair who passed two meters in height at sixteen. He wears the close-fitting black suit typical of the City of Silver.

About

Earnest young warrior of the City of Silver who carries the Sun's light into a cursed land.

Backstory

Derrick Berg grew up in the City of Silver inside the cursed Forsaken Land of the Gods, where the curse even forced him to kill his own parents. In autumn 1349 a new mask appeared above the Everstage: young and lonely, with a thin gold band across the brow. When Nika drew its owner in, the name that flashed through her mind was Sun Wukong. He was a boy who had never seen the sun. He paid for the Troupe's help with historical records and Beyonder materials taken from local monsters, advanced along the Sun pathway, and learned a great deal from the other guests, above all Odysseus. He gradually earned the trust of Chief Colin Iliad. In about 1353 he led the first expedition out of the Forsaken Land, bringing his people and Moon City into the world above. That also opened the way for Max to march out with him. The leaving fight was against Sasrir. The City of Silver's expedition squad did the fighting, Colin and Derrick among them, and Nika fought alongside them; she couldn't land the killing blow, so she helped however she could. Fors watched to record the powers, and Max protected her. Colin died there, and Derrick took his seat on the Six-member Council.

Sequence 6: Notary

    After Amanises uses Antigonus to divert the Curtain of Fool characteristics into the Forsaken Land of the Gods, the third transmigrator (Zhou Mingrui / Klein Moretti) perishes upon arrival, triggering the release of the fourth cocoon: 22-year-old Polish stage technician Weronika "Nika" Sparrow. Because she bowed on an empty theatre stage at the end of her luck ritual on Earth, the unsealed 10th Sefirah—the Everstage—claims her before the Celestial Worthy's net can bind her to the Fool pathway. She awakens in Backlund in the body of 18-year-old Lady Viola Veronica Marlowe seconds after a Secret Order strike team (a Marionettist, a Magician, and a Clown) raids Viscount Marlowe's manor for a Sequence 4 Bizarro Sorcerer characteristic. Waking in a pool of blood with her fatal head wound healing and a silver-white silk streak in her red hair, Nika grabs a revolver and shoots the Secret Order Clown dead as the Nighthawks arrive. A few nights later, she repeats the ritual in an empty theatre, bows to the dark seats, hears a single clap, and steps onto the Everstage for the first time as the Prompter—recruiting Audrey Hall and Alger Wilson (Odysseus) into the Troupe
