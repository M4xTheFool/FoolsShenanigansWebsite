Alista Tudor's Red Priest Apotheosis

728, Fourth Epoch, Death, Battle, Advancement, Northern Continent (Tudor Empire - Backlund)

Death, Battle, Advancement

What Happened

When Black Emperor Solomon revives and forces Princes of Abolition Alista Tudor and Trunsoest into a corner, Alista Tudor refuses to surrender his Sequence 1 characteristic. Aided by Adam, Amon, and the Primordial Demoness Cheek, Alista Tudor lures War Angel Medici alongside the Red Priest angels of the Sauron and Einhorn families into a trap in Backlund. Devouring Medici's Red Priest Uniqueness and Sequence 1 Conqueror characteristics, Alista Tudor forcibly switches from Sequence 1: Prince of Abolition (Lawyer pathway) to Sequence 0: Red Priest (Hunter pathway)—becoming the half-mad Blood Emperor and Max's greatest rival on the Hunter pathway.
