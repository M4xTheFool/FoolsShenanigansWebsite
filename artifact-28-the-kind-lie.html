The Kind Lie

Sealed Artifact A-028, Arbiter (Justiciar) pathway, Sequence 2 level, mixed with the Marauder pathway

Mixed characteristics

Characteristics

  • Arbiter (Justiciar) pathway, Sequence 2 (its main one)
  • Marauder (Error) pathway, Sequence 3

Appearance

Plain black, older-looking than it is. Two tiny weights at the throat always hang level, and nobody remembers its colour the same way twice.

Ability

A sheath made to seal and deceive The Lady's Favour. Its Balancer half holds the sword's hunger level; its Mentor of Deceit half makes the sword believe it has already been fed and that the hand holding it must never be harmed. The deception holds as long as the sword comes within 10 metres of the sheath at least once every 2 minutes.

Drawback

A lie has to be kept up. If the sheath is lost or broken, the deception ends all at once: the sword learns the truth, the 2-minute count begins, and when it starts to drain, it collects everything it was owed, faster than it would have otherwise. Being made from an Error characteristic, the sheath also calls to Error Beyonders, and those who feed on such characteristics can smell it.

Notes

A Sheathe create by Max to fully Seal the Lady's Favour.

Held By
