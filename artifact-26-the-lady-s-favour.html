The Lady’s Favour

Sealed Artifact A-026, Lawyer (Black Emperor) pathway, Sequence 1 level, mixed with the Sleepless pathway

Mixed characteristics

Characteristics

  • Lawyer (Black Emperor) pathway, Sequence 1 (its main one)
  • Sleepless (Darkness) pathway, Sequence 4

Appearance

A pristine looking sword made of dark iron that reflects things incorrectly; Made with one side of the guard longer than the other; It has a cloth with stars that seem to be moving on it;
It reflects Max truthfully, bending the surroundings and everything else;

Ability

The sword bends and shatters whatever it strikes, redefining definitions as it does: striking a substitute strikes the true body, and the Order around it bends. It affects reality on a deep level. It can exploit cracks and imperfections to break and shatter things. Where the cracks run deep enough, as in someone far weaker than its holder or someone already broken, it can kill simply by being drawn and its holder wishing it; anyone stronger still has to be cut. It distorts space: a stroke may look like a miss, but the cut still appears. It can amplify its wielder's abilities and distort enemies with a thought, or on its own. By its presence alone it heavily weakens enemies and strengthens allies.

Drawback

Unless it is sealed and deceived by a special sheath, it drains its wielder of blood, spirituality and flesh at an incredibly fast rate. It must come within 10 metres of the sheath at least once every 2 minutes. If it doesn't, it begins to drain its wielder, and once the wielder is dead, everything within 2 metres of him.

Notes

A gift from Amanises to Ante: A sword befitting a King - the same King from all those years ago and a complitely new one.

Held By

  • Max (C-016)
