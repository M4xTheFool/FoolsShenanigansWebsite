An ancient castle floating above an endless gray fog. Whoever controls it can pull others' spirit bodies into it, hear prayers addressed to it and keep what happens there hidden from divination. Klein Moretti met the Tarot Club there. Its Great Old One, the Lord of the Mysteries, is one of the Three Pillars that stand above all the other Great Old Ones (Ch. 1347).

Above the Sequence

Symbolism
Mysteries, the gray fog above reality, the Spirit World, and space and time.
  • Lord of the Mysteries: Holds authority over every mystery, everything hidden and everything unknown.
  • Dominator of the Spirit World: Rules the Spirit World and the spirit bodies that drift through it.
  • King of Space-Time: Commands space and time, as the full title on the Second Blasphemy Slate says.
  • Castle above the Gray Fog: Pulls spirit bodies into Sefirah Castle, hears every prayer addressed to it and hides what happens there from divination.

Held By

  • Celestial Worthy of Heaven and Earth for Blessings (C-044), Above the Sequence

Notes

Full title on the Second Blasphemy Slate: Lord of the Mysteries, King of Space-Time, Embodiment of Sefirah Castle, Dominator of the Spirit World (Ch. 1345). Its Great Old One also needs the Attendant of Mysteries, Worm of Time and Key of Stars characteristics. After Amon's defeat the Error and Door Uniquenesses gather to Klein, who is called equivalent to half a Lord of the Mysteries (Ch. 1387, 1392). The symbolism and Great Old One abilities are an interpretation of the full title and the description; the novel never lists them.
