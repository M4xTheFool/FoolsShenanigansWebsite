Sequences

Sequence 9: Sleepless

  • Little Sleep: Stays fully alert on only a few hours of sleep.
  • Night Empowerment: Grows stronger, faster and sharper as the night deepens.
  • Dark Vision: Sees clearly in darkness.
  • Spirit Sense: Has high spirituality, a sense for danger, and can perform simple rituals and divination.

Sequence 8: Midnight Poet

  • Midnight Poems: Recites poems or songs that calm, frighten, pacify or soothe listeners.
  • Lulling Verse: Uses chanted verse to wear down resistance and send groups of people to sleep.
  • Night Fighter: Gains better combat training and a stronger boost from the night.

Sequence 7: Nightmare

  • Dream Entry: Enters, observes and reshapes the dreams of sleepers nearby.
  • Nightmare: Pulls targets into sleep-like dreams and attacks their minds with terrifying visions.
  • Dream Interrogation: Draws memories and truths out of a dreamer by steering the dream.

Sequence 6: Soul Assurer

  • Requiem: Lulls a spirit into deep stillness, suppressing thought, emotion, intuition and movement.
  • Soul Soothing: Calms and protects the souls of companions against mental strain and attack.
  • Spirit Pacifying: Quiets ghosts and silences restless undead.
  • Spirit Perception: Directly perceives spiritual beings, with stronger ritual and divination skill.

Sequence 5: Spirit Warlock

  • Spirit Command: Commands spirits as obedient servants.
  • Spirit Sealing: Seals natural, evil or dead spirits inside its body, keeping them dormant until needed.
  • Spirit Summoning: Calls sealed spirits out to fight, scout, track, fly or shield it.

Sequence 4: Nightwatcher

  • Domain of Night: Spreads pure darkness that empowers it and its spirits while weakening and draining enemies.
  • Concealment: Hides its appearance, identity and mystical connections from sight and divination.
  • Curse of Misfortune: Sours a target's luck so accidents and failures follow it.
  • Demigod Form: Gains a demigod's body and an incomplete mythical creature form.

Sequence 3: Horror Bishop

  • Horror: Radiates an aura of terror that can paralyze, unhinge or even kill weaker beings.
  • Nightmare World: Drags everyone across a small city into a shared dream that it controls.
  • Sword of Darkness: Forms a blade of darkness whose silent waves dissolve whatever they touch.

Sequence 2: Servant of Concealment

  • Concealment Authority: Hides people, places, actions and truths, even from divination and prophecy.
  • Unveiling: Strips away concealment that others have cast.
  • Concealed World: Creates a hidden realm where battles leave the outside untouched and secrets cannot be spoken.
  • Angelic Form: Becomes an angel with a complete mythical creature form.

Sequence 1: Knight of Misfortune

  • Misfortune Authority: Brings bad luck, accidents and disasters on individuals, nations or whole continents.
  • Return from Darkness: Revives after death through the darkness, though each return risks merging with it.
  • Spirit Legions: Commands vast numbers of spirits at once.

Sequence 0: Darkness

  • Authority of Night: Rules night, darkness and sleep, granting dreams, repose or eternal slumber.
  • Divine Concealment: Erases traces, identities and connections, or hides entire realms from the world.
  • Misfortune and Horror: Spreads catastrophe and terror on a divine scale.
  • Eternal Night: Cannot be permanently killed while darkness remains.
  • Lord of Spirits: Commands the spirits of nature.

Above the Sequence: Eternal Darkness

A Great Old One holds River of Eternal Darkness and the Uniqueness of every pathway it gathers.

Symbolism
Darkness, death, the underworld, and space and time folded into one.
  • Eternal Darkness: Covers everything in a darkness that swallows light, sound and life.
  • River of the Dead: Rules the underworld where the dead go, through the river's many tributaries.
  • Space-Time-in-One: Folds space and time into a single point, as the full title says.
