Salinger's Blood Banner

Sealed Artifact A-025, Hunter (Red Priest) pathway, Sequence 0 level

Appearance

A charred banner. The flagpole is iron-black metal, and there are a large number of dangerous blood spots on the banner.

Ability

The Red Priest Uniqueness. A charred, bloodstained banner on an iron-black flagpole. Accommodating it is the last step before Sequence 0 Red Priest.

Drawback

The underground mausoleum sealing the 0-01 needs eternal, deathly darkness. It can occasionally have light, but there can't be continuous light in the same place. When Beyonders above Sequence 5 with godhood aspects approach 0-01, 0-01 instinctively burns the area, bringing embers that can last for a period of time. This phenomena can cause the seal of 0-01 to lose balance and weaken, inducing the corruption of 0-01 to strengthen and spread all over Morora

Notes

Officially Sealed Artifact 0-01, sealed beneath the exiled city of Morora. It passed from Salinger to Herabergen at Salinger's death. During the God of Combat's war with Amanises, Herabergen grudgingly handed it to Max, who called in the favours of every still-living god in his debt; Amanises had promised him he'd get it if he wagered them. Max accommodated it soon after, so it stopped existing as a Sealed Artifact.
Sealing:
Place it in an underground mausoleum with a large number of soldier mannequins. Construct a cemetery with more than a million corpses above it, supplemented by a real city with a population of more than 100,000.
