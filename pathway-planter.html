Sequences

Sequence 9: Planter

  • Farming Mastery: Expertly tends seeds, crops and soil and handles farm tools to bring in healthy harvests.
  • Physical Enhancement: Gains greatly increased strength and stamina.
  • Weather Reading: Predicts coming weather by reading clouds, wind and other natural signs.

Sequence 8: Doctor

  • Healing: Treats injuries and illnesses of both body and soul, including ailments caused by evil or corruption.
  • Surgical Mastery: Performs skilled surgery, from suturing severed limbs to transplanting organs.
  • Soul Suture: Operates directly on the soul to mend wounds, remove curses and corruption, or attack it.
  • Diagnostic Vision: Reads a patient's health precisely, spotting hidden illness from outward signs.
  • Giving Life: Gradually restores strength and vigor to the weak and exhausted.

Sequence 7: Harvest Priest

  • Seed Catalysis: Speeds the growth of seeds and plants, even raising thorny vines to entangle enemies.
  • Plant and Insect Command: Directs mindless plants and insects within about thirty meters to aid the priest.
  • Weather Rituals: Performs rituals that clear the skies or call down rain for the crops.
  • Blessing of the Land: Healing now extends to crops, fields and land, curing blights and plant diseases.

Sequence 6: Biologist

  • Cross-breeding: Creates chimeras and entirely new species by combining animals, plants and even objects.
  • Poison Creation: The body produces potent toxins that can be released through hair or other means.
  • Knowledge of Life: Deeply understands the traits and inner workings of living organisms.

Sequence 5: Druid

  • Wrath of Nature: Turns the surroundings hostile against up to fifty chosen enemies.
  • Swamp: Turns the ground within thirty meters into a swamp that traps or buries foes.
  • Underground Travel: Softens the earth to sink into it and travel underground.
  • Child of the Oak: Covers the body in protective bark or stone skin.
  • Earth Golems: Builds golems of stone and earth to fight or labor.
  • Animal Shape: Transforms into a giant bear or similar beast, though without the creature's special powers.

Sequence 4: Classical Alchemist

  • Artificial Life: Creates golems and long-lived artificial humans from soul fragments and raw materials.
  • Mutation: Attacks warp a target's body and mind and push them toward madness.
  • Creature Command: Controls low-intelligence Beyonder creatures and briefly calms their insanity.
  • Life Aura: Fills the surroundings with life force, making plants, animals and people grow rapidly.
  • Restoring Touch: Heals anyone by touch and can briefly return reason to those who lost control.
  • Mythical Form: Can take a partial, incomplete Mythical Creature form, at a cost.

Sequence 3: Pallbearer

  • Evil Spirit Conversion: Turns the dead within ten kilometers into Evil Spirits that serve the Pallbearer.
  • Life Deprivation: Every blow puts the target through a trial of death that may kill instantly and otherwise drains life.
  • Death Descent: Drains the life of targets in range without touching them.
  • Return to Earth: Dissolves undead within ten kilometers back into the soil.
  • Maternal Embrace: Seals even undead Angels away within a coffin and the earth.
  • Clay Golems: Gives life to up to three golems of soil or clay with demigod strength.

Sequence 2: Desolate Matriarch

  • Fertility and Barrenness: Makes land for hundreds of kilometers burst with life, or turns it barren so crops wither.
  • Desolation: Drains all life within thirty kilometers and poisons a wider area, pushing Beyonders toward losing control.
  • Legion of the Dead: Turns every dead human within fifty kilometers into an Evil Spirit, some as strong as demigods.
  • Great Golems: Builds dolls and golems from soil, plants, lava, ore and metal, up to nine of demigod strength.
  • Mythical Creature Form: Becomes a complete mythical creature, a mass of flesh, organs, wheat, mushrooms and vines.
  • Feminine Change: The potion turns a male drinker into a woman.

Sequence 1: Naturewalker

  • Child of Nature: Reshapes the environment and merges with natural elements to move and fight within them.
  • Nature's Command: Commands natural forces such as fire, lightning and even the light of the sun and moon.
  • World Creation: Nurtures a realm of its own that embodies nature itself.
  • Authority of Nature: Holds partial authority over nature and motherhood, empowering every earlier ability.

Sequence 0: Mother

  • Motherhood: Gives birth to all things and can awaken life even in the inanimate.
  • Earth: As the origin and resting place of all things, rules the cycle of life, death and return.
  • Nature: Has near-indestructible vitality and raises plant barriers able to hold back True Gods.
  • Life and Death: Makes creatures grow and mutate uncontrollably, or drains the life even of gods.
  • Bountiful Earth: Rules a fertile divine kingdom, with lesser authority over harvest, healing and desolation.

Above the Sequence: Goddess of Origins

A Great Old One holds Brood Hive and the Uniqueness of every pathway it gathers.

Symbolism
Life, birth and growth, and the filth and decay that come with them.
  • Mother of All: Gives birth to and shapes life of every kind.
  • Brood Hive of Filth: Spreads corruption, rot and decay through living things.
