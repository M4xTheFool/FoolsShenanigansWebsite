Sequences

Sequence 9: Corpse Collector

  • Corpse Immunity: Shrugs off corpse poison, rot and the deathly aura of cadavers.
  • Undead Insight: Spots the weak points of undead creatures.
  • Cold Resistance: Barely feels cold and runs at a low body temperature.
  • Spirit Vision: Sees ghosts and spirits, many of which treat it as harmless.
  • Corpse Lore: Skilled at examining bodies and telling unnatural deaths from natural ones.

Sequence 8: Gravedigger

  • Burial Rites: Knows the funeral rites that lay the dead to rest and keep them from rising.
  • Undead Fighter: Gains greater strength, agility and techniques for fighting the undead.
  • Spirit Talk: Speaks with nearby spirits and gives them simple orders, such as holding a target.
  • Eye of Death: Finds the vital spiritual nodes of unfamiliar undead and monsters with no normal anatomy.

Sequence 7: Spirit Medium

  • Spirit Communication: Sees and speaks with the spirits of the dead and of nature.
  • Spirit Channeling: Borrows nearby spirits to learn information or to chill, bind and track targets.
  • Soul Channeling: Contacts recently departed souls, or with more risk, the spirit of a living person.
  • Deathly State: Mimics an undead's spiritual state to resist cold, decay and death energy.

Sequence 6: Spirit Guide

  • Language of the Dead: Speaks words that act directly on spirits, drawing souls from bodies or controlling them.
  • Spirit Command: Guides and commands spirits and ownerless undead in large numbers.
  • Raise Dead: Animates corpses as mindless zombies and skeletons.

Sequence 5: Gatekeeper

  • Underworld Gate: Senses and opens doors to the underworld that suck targets in.
  • Grasp of the Dead: Its gates release undead hands that seize enemies and drag them into the realm of the dead.
  • Inner Underworld: Stores many souls, spirits and undead inside its body as a hidden army.

Sequence 4: Undying

  • Undying Body: Survives fatal wounds; only complete destruction of the body truly threatens it.
  • Half-Dead Existence: Exists between body and spirit and can travel through the spirit world.
  • Withering Wind: Unleashes a deathly wind that withers living things.
  • Death Spells: Casts death magic and can seal dangerous objects.
  • Demigod Form: Gains a demigod's power and an incomplete mythical creature form.

Sequence 3: Ferryman

  • Soul Ferrying: Carries souls across the river of the dead to their final end.
  • Death Gaze: A look that kills ordinary beings and withers stronger ones.
  • Hands of Life and Death: Its left hand pulls targets toward death while its right can return life.
  • Already Dead: Shrugs off most attacks other than purification, since the dead cannot die again.

Sequence 2: Death Consul

  • Angelic Form: Becomes an angel with a complete mythical creature form.
  • Nation of the Dead: Creates a city-sized domain where weak life dies instantly and stronger beings slowly wither.
  • Absolute Death: Drains vitality from an area and blocks most resurrection and substitution tricks.
  • Judge of the Dead: Judges and enslaves the dead, commanding vast undead armies.

Sequence 1: Pale Emperor

  • Pale World: Drains all color from an area, rapidly weathering objects and weakening color-based powers.
  • Death Decree: Sentences anyone it sees or points at to death.
  • Soul Enslavement: Pulls out Spirit Bodies and enslaves any living being that has a soul.
  • Soul Suppression: Crushes souls, snuffing out thought and vitality.

Sequence 0: Death

  • Authority of Death: Rules death and the underworld, the final destination of all life.
  • Conceptual Death: Kills not only life but memories, connections, substitutes and paths of resurrection.
  • Master of Undead: Source and lord of all undead, able to enslave anything with a soul.
  • Pallor: Silences mind, will, color and supernatural power.

Above the Sequence: Eternal Darkness

A Great Old One holds River of Eternal Darkness and the Uniqueness of every pathway it gathers.

Symbolism
Darkness, death, the underworld, and space and time folded into one.
  • Eternal Darkness: Covers everything in a darkness that swallows light, sound and life.
  • River of the Dead: Rules the underworld where the dead go, through the river's many tributaries.
  • Space-Time-in-One: Folds space and time into a single point, as the full title says.
