Sequences

Sequence 9: Reader

  • Speed Reading: Gets through books and documents at great speed and remembers nearly everything read.
  • Rapid Learning: Picks up new subjects, languages and skills far faster than ordinary people.
  • Clear Focus: Concentrates and understands with unusual clarity, turning heavy reading into usable knowledge.
  • Ritual Spirituality: Gains enough spirituality to perform basic ritualistic magic.

Sequence 8: Student of Ratiocination

  • Ratiocination: Builds sound chains of logical deduction from small clues to reach conclusions.
  • Keen Observation: Notices overlooked details, inconsistencies and contradictions in scenes, accounts and behaviour.
  • Ordered Memory: Sorts stored knowledge so the right facts surface quickly while reasoning.

Sequence 7: Detective

  • Clue Hunting: Finds hidden traces and evidence that others walk straight past.
  • Seeing Through Lies: Spots lies, disguises and false stories from small tells and contradictions.
  • Scene Reconstruction: Pieces together past events at a location from lingering traces, unless those were erased or falsified.
  • Field Training: Gains better agility, weapon skill and command of ritual magic for dangerous cases.

Sequence 6: Polymath

  • Broad Mastery: Rapidly masters many mundane skills and wide fields of mystical knowledge.
  • Analysis: Studies a Beyonder power it witnesses to work out its pathway, principles, strengths and weaknesses.
  • Imitation: Recreates a weaker version of a correctly analyzed Beyonder power; a flawed analysis can backfire.

Sequence 5: Mysticism Magister

  • Ritual Mastery: Performs rituals and applies mystical knowledge freely, reliably and with little preparation.
  • Mystic Arts: Casts a broad range of spells for attack, defense, healing, sealing and investigation.
  • Spell Invention: Designs new spells from symbolism, materials and careful experimentation.
  • Stronger Imitation: Copied Beyonder powers become noticeably stronger, though still weaker than the originals.

Sequence 4: Prophet

  • Prophecy: Glimpses the futures most likely to happen, though what it shows is probability rather than fixed fate.
  • Demigod Body: Gains a demigod's physique and spirituality along with an incomplete mythical creature form.
  • Deeper Analysis: Analyzes and imitates Beyonder powers more thoroughly and accurately than before.

Sequence 3: Cognizer

  • Cognition: Perceives the true essence, structure, symbols and hidden workings of a target or Beyonder power.
  • Faithful Imitation: Reproduces the Beyonder powers it has come to understand far more closely than before.
  • Principle Leverage: Finds the single point where a thing's underlying principles can be exploited with minimal force.
  • Symbol Spells: Derives a working spell after grasping the meaning of a mystical symbol.

Sequence 2: Wisdom Angel

  • Wisdom: When forced to choose, foresees how events will shift and picks the most correct action at the right moment.
  • Angelic Form: Becomes an angel with the complete White Tower mythical creature form.
  • Heightened Insight: Learning, analysis and prophecy sharpen until the weaknesses of foes and plans lie bare.
  • Near-Perfect Imitation: Imitated powers come close to the original's full strength, though authorities stay out of reach.

Sequence 1: Omniscient Eye

  • Omniscient Eye: Watches distant targets, learning their past, present state, likely futures, secrets and weaknesses.
  • Equal Imitation: Imitated powers match the original's strength, though not its authority or symbolism.
  • Astral Tampering: Briefly alters one aspect of a concept in the astral world so an action fails, errs or intensifies.

Sequence 0: White Tower

  • Authority of Knowledge: Holds divine authority over knowledge, wisdom and analysis.
  • Omniscience: Perceives knowledge about almost anything directly, without needing to consult the spirit world.
  • Divine Imitation: Imitates any power it understands at full strength and can refine or surpass it.
  • Principle Mastery: Exploits the foundations of reality and crafts spells that others can cast.

Above the Sequence: God Almighty

A Great Old One holds Chaos Sea and the Uniqueness of every pathway it gathers.

Symbolism
Creation, the source of matter and energy, and the place where the real and the illusory merge.
  • Omnipotent and Omniscient: Sees and can do almost anything within the Chaos Sea's reach, as the full title says.
  • Source of Matter and Energy: Draws matter and energy out of the Chaos Sea to make whatever it needs.
  • Real and Illusory: Merges the real and the imagined, so what it pictures can become real.
