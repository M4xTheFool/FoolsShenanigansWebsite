Abilities

Sequence 9: Prisoner

  • Self-Restraint: Suppresses urges, hunger, pain and fear, staying composed under temptation or confinement.
  • Unbending Will: Resists being controlled or manipulated, holding firm against outside influence on the mind.
  • Physical Enhancement: Gains greater strength, endurance and pain tolerance.

Sequence 8: Lunatic

  • Frenzy: Lets madness take hold to gain raw strength, speed and pain tolerance at the cost of judgment.
  • Madman's Intuition: Unhinged thinking sharpens a sense of danger and hostility around them.
  • Physical Enhancement: The body grows markedly stronger.

Sequence 7: Werewolf

  • Wolf Transformation: Turns partly or fully into a wolf-like beast with claws, fangs and great strength.
  • Regeneration: Heals wounds rapidly and shrugs off ordinary injuries.
  • Predator Senses: Gains a wolf's keen nose and hearing for tracking blood, fear and movement.

Sequence 6: Zombie

  • Zombie Body: Becomes cold, pale and near-undead, resisting bullets, poison and disease.
  • Undying Endurance: Feels little pain, barely needs air and survives wounds that would kill the living.
  • Command Zombies: Raises and controls zombies as servants.
  • Deathly Chill: Gives off a corpse-like cold that unsettles those nearby.

Sequence 5: Wraith

  • Incorporeal Form: Becomes a ghostly body that passes through walls and objects, flies and turns invisible.
  • Possession: Enters and takes over another person's body, using it as its own.
  • Wraith Scream: Lets out a shriek that strikes the spirit, causing terror, confusion or unconsciousness.
  • Mirror Travel: Hides in and passes through mirrors and other reflective surfaces.
  • Ghost Command: Controls lesser ghosts and spirits.

Sequence 4: Puppet

  • Object Control: Animates and commands inanimate objects within a radius, like a poltergeist.
  • Puppetry: Turns possessed or captured victims into puppets whose bodies it moves at will.
  • Puppet Body: Gains a doll-like, resilient body that is very hard to destroy.
  • Mythical Form: Can take an incomplete Mythical Creature form as a demigod.

Sequence 3: Disciple of Silence

  • Source of Curses: Curses people through any link to them, such as belongings, blood or a name.
  • Cursed Artifact: Can become like a living cursed object that afflicts whoever touches or uses it.
  • Silence: Smothers sound, speech and spellcasting within an area.

Sequence 2: Ancient Bane

  • Ancient Curses: Lays lasting curses that can spread through bloodlines, groups or whole regions.
  • Mythical Creature Form: Becomes a complete mythical creature built around an ancient evil relic that anchors its curses.
  • Corrupting Presence: Its influence warps the bodies and minds of those nearby toward monstrous forms.

Events

  1. 28 June 1349
    Nika's Awakening & First Curtain CallBattle and Advancement
    Backlund (Marlowe Manor & Empty Theatre)
    With Nika (Awakens), Klein Moretti (dies), Amanises, Viscount Marlowe, Fors Wall, Audrey Hall, Alger Wilson, Emlyn White, Cattleya, Leonard Mitchell, Xio Derecha, Sharron and Derrick Berg

    After Amanises uses Antigonus to divert the Curtain of Fool characteristics into the Forsaken Land of the Gods, the third transmigrator (Zhou Mingrui / Klein Moretti) perishes upon arrival, triggering the release of the fourth cocoon: 22-year-old Polish stage technician Weronika "Nika" Sparrow. Because she bowed on an empty theatre stage at the end of her luck ritual on Earth, the unsealed 10th Sefirah—the Everstage—claims her before the Celestial Worthy's net can bind her to the Fool pathway. She awakens in Backlund in the body of 18-year-old Lady Viola Veronica Marlowe seconds after a Secret Order strike team (a Marionettist, a Magician, and a Clown) raids Viscount Marlowe's manor for a Sequence 4 Bizarro Sorcerer characteristic. Waking in a pool of blood with her fatal head wound healing and a silver-white silk streak in her red hair, Nika grabs a revolver and shoots the Secret Order Clown dead as the Nighthawks arrive. A few nights later, she repeats the ritual in an empty theatre, bows to the dark seats, hears a single clap, and steps onto the Everstage for the first time as the Prompter—recruiting Audrey Hall and Alger Wilson (Odysseus) into the Troupe

  2. 31 December 1360
    Truth PlayAdvancement
    Everstage and the entire world
    With Max (Troupe and Angel), Fors Wall (Troupe), Audrey Hall (Troupe), Alger Wilson (Troupe), Derrick Berg (Troupe), Emlyn White (Troupe), Cattleya (Troupe), Xio Derecha (Troupe), Nika (Main Actor), Sharron (Troupe), Leonard Mitchell (Troupe), Adam (Honoured Guest), Amanises (Honoured Guest), Pallez Zoroast (Angel and Honoured Guest), Will Auceptin (Angel and Honoured Guest), Azik Eggers (Angel and Honoured Guest) and Bernadette Gustav (Honoured Guest)

    The now Maestro Nika does a play on the Everstage - with every member of the Troupe present. As well as some honour guests like Amanises and Adam in which 'she' shares the truth with the Troupe - there never was a Phantom other than 'Her'. She shares some of 'Her' old life in the Pre-Epoch. Talks about each of the Troupe members accomplishments - talks about all 'Her' angels - Max, Will Auceptin, Pallez, Azik - while slightly bending the truth to make them look stronger, more refined. Thanks all the honoured guests for helping 'Her' - even Adam who was an enemy for some time. Hopes that the Troupe members will stay with 'Her' still and that they will all grow before the Apocalypse arrives. After one last bow and during great applause 'She' advances to the Sovereign of Strings.
