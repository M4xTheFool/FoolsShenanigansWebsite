Sequences

Sequence 9: Spectator

  • Keen Observation: Reads body language, expressions and habits to infer emotions and intentions.
  • Quiet Observer: Watches from the sidelines while drawing little attention to itself.
  • Sharp Mind: Gains better memory, deduction, eyesight and emotional control.

Sequence 8: Telepathist

  • Surface Thoughts: Senses a person's current thoughts and feelings.
  • Thought Prediction: Simulates how a person is likely to think and react next.
  • Lie Sense: Notices lies and hidden reactions through spiritual perception.
  • Natural Rapport: Knows which words and expressions will feel natural to a given audience.

Sequence 7: Psychiatrist

  • Placate: Calms fear, madness and the early signs of losing control.
  • Frenzy: Inflames emotions into rage or madness, harming mind and spirit.
  • Awe: Fills a target with dread or submission.
  • Psychological Cue: Plants a suggestion that the target believes is their own decision.
  • Mind Contact: Reaches into a mind that is partly open to it.

Sequence 6: Hypnotist

  • Hypnosis: Hypnotizes a person, a group or itself, leaving instructions that act later unnoticed.
  • Battle Hypnosis: Makes foes hesitate, retreat, grow confused or attack their allies.
  • Psychological Invisibility: Becomes a blind spot that observers' minds simply ignore.
  • Dragon Scales: Grows tough scales that defend against physical harm.

Sequence 5: Dreamwalker

  • Dream Entry: Enters, watches and reshapes other people's dreams.
  • Dream Travel: Moves between nearby sleepers' dreams and hides inside them.
  • Dream Influence: Slowly shifts a dreamer's feelings, loyalties, fears or habits.
  • Secret Digging: Guides dream imagery to reveal what a dreamer is hiding.

Sequence 4: Manipulator

  • Subconscious Sea: Enters the sea of collective subconscious to invade minds and read deep thoughts.
  • Hidden Commands: Buries ideas and orders in minds, leaving almost no trace.
  • Mind Plague: Spreads mental illness through a whole community.
  • Mind Storm: Batters mental defenses with hostile, corrupting thoughts.
  • Virtual Personas: Splits off separate personas that guard its mind or infiltrate others.
  • Mind Dragon Form: Becomes a demigod with an incomplete Mind Dragon mythical form.

Sequence 3: Dream Weaver

  • Lifelike Dreams: Weaves dreams so real that fear or wounds inside them can break or kill.
  • Plague Storm: Spreads desire, unstable emotion and corruption across an area.
  • Consciousness Stroll: Carries its physical body through the collective subconscious to travel.
  • Living Personas: Its virtual personas become independent beings that act and grow on their own.

Sequence 2: Discerner

  • Dream Maze: Links minds through the collective subconscious into a maze that drags victims through their secrets and fears.
  • Discernment: Sees weaknesses, hidden fears, motives and causes, even past ordinary anti-divination.
  • Envisioned Form: Temporarily takes on a form or state it truly understands and believes in.
  • Angelic Form: Completes the Mind Dragon form and becomes an angel.

Sequence 1: Author

  • Narrative Writing: Writes stories that come true when their events unfold logically.
  • Arranged Coincidence: Sets up chance meetings and coincidences that steer events.
  • Name Sense: Notices when its name or title is spoken, and can draw those who know it into its stories.
  • Living Concepts: Blends dreams with reality and creates mental concepts that act on their own.

Sequence 0: Visionary

  • Envisioning: Whatever it truly imagines can become real, from beings and objects to a divine kingdom.
  • Ruler of Minds: Creates or erases personality, memory, emotion and sanity.
  • Declared Future: Speaks a future that unfolds when backed by enough coherent development.
  • Loss of Control: Can shatter another Beyonder's mental balance.

Above the Sequence: God Almighty

A Great Old One holds Chaos Sea and the Uniqueness of every pathway it gathers.

Symbolism
Creation, the source of matter and energy, and the place where the real and the illusory merge.
  • Omnipotent and Omniscient: Sees and can do almost anything within the Chaos Sea's reach, as the full title says.
  • Source of Matter and Energy: Draws matter and energy out of the Chaos Sea to make whatever it needs.
  • Real and Illusory: Merges the real and the imagined, so what it pictures can become real.
