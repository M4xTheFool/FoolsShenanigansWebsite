Sequences

Sequence 9: Criminal

  • Criminal Proficiency: Knows a convict's tricks and can kill efficiently with almost any weapon at hand.
  • Physical Enhancement: Gains a tougher, stronger body built for violence.
  • Criminal Instincts: Keen instincts warn of danger and sniff out opportunities for crime.

Sequence 8: Unwinged Angel

  • Demonic Traits: The body changes in inhuman ways, granting two or three minor devil-like spells that vary per person.
  • Cold Blood: Feels little guilt or empathy, staying calm and ruthless in the face of violence.
  • Physical Enhancement: Strength and toughness rise further.

Sequence 7: Serial Killer

  • Devil Worship: Masters the rituals and ritual magic used to worship and please devils.
  • Devil's Favor: Ritual killings earn a devil's favor that can later summon a devil's projection for aid.
  • Cold Intellect: Gains sharper intelligence and mental focus for planning crimes.

Sequence 6: Devil

  • Danger Premonition: Senses imminent threats and where they come from before they strike.
  • Sulfur Flames: Conjures poisonous flames and exploding sulfur fireballs that burn and poison targets.
  • Corruption: Unleashes an area attack that afflicts victims with symptoms of the Abyss's corruption.
  • Devil Spells: Numbs and slows nearby foes, or clots a victim's blood with a potentially lethal spell.
  • Demonization: Takes a true devil form with armored skin, greater strength and speed, and immunity to most poisons.
  • Devil Bloodline: Gains racial traits that vary by devil species, such as escaping death by leaving only a shadow behind.

Sequence 5: Desire Apostle

  • Desire Mastery: Seizes and inflames the emotions and desires of distant targets, driving them toward ruin.
  • Defiling Seeds: Plants seeds of corruption in an emotionally shaken target that fester into depravity or death.
  • Avatar of Desire: Turns into an illusory black liquid that is hard to harm or track and sways enemies' emotions.

Sequence 4: Demon

  • Mind Fog: Slowly dulls the thinking of everyone nearby without them noticing.
  • Desire Illusions: Twists desires through its actions, causing illusions that shatter sanity or trigger loss of control.
  • Long Premonition: Danger sense sometimes warns of threats two or three days in advance.
  • Partial Demonization: Manifests part of its mythical form on one body part for sustained power.
  • Cunning Mind: Becomes cold, sharp and sly, with a demigod's intellect.

Sequence 3: Blatherer

  • Blathering: Its words corrupt listeners' minds, causing confusion, injury, curses or loss of control.
  • Distant Whispers: Its murmurs can reach targets far away and bring about sudden death.
  • Prayer Response: Can hear and answer prayers within a certain range.

Sequence 2: Bloody Archduke

  • Blood Form: Becomes corrosive blood that makes opponents bleed from every orifice until they die.
  • Fear: Wields core authority over fear.
  • Seed of Malice: Plants an evolved seed of corruption that erodes the mind and can be used to revive or kill.
  • Desire Awakening: Draws out a target's deepest desires until they show signs of losing control.
  • Mythical Creature Form: Has fully become a mythical creature of the Abyss.

Sequence 1: Filthy Monarch

  • Filth: Rules filth and corruption, defiling bodies, minds and places.
  • Corrosion: Eats away matter, power and will alike.
  • Pollution: Spreads contamination that taints and degrades everything it touches.

Sequence 0: Abyss

  • Authority of Depravity: Rules degeneration and desire, dragging beings down into corruption.
  • Curses and Mutation: Holds authority over curses and twisted mutations.
  • Fear and Malice: Embodies fear and malice itself.
  • Malice Incarnate: Becomes a black liquid that slips into bodies, minds and souls as a Seed of Malice, travelling between hosts.

Above the Sequence: Father of Devils

A Great Old One holds Tenebrous World and the Uniqueness of every pathway it gathers.

Symbolism
Evil, desire, curses and everything deviant.
  • Source of Curses: Curses are born from it and answer to it.
  • Lord of Deviants: Rules devils, the depraved and everything that strays from what is right.
