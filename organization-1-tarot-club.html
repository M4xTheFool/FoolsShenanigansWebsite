In the original novel, the Tarot Club met above the gray fog in Sefirah Castle from early July 1349. Klein Moretti sat in the seat of the Fool, and every member took a tarot card as a codename, beginning with Justice and the Hanged Man. In this story it never forms. On 28 June 1349 the Evernight Goddess diverted the Curtain meant for the third transmigrator into the Forsaken Land of the Gods, and Zhou Mingrui died on arrival in Klein's body. The castle above the gray fog stayed empty, so no one was there to pull Audrey Hall and Alger Wilson in. A few nights later Nika bowed in an empty theatre and stepped onto the Everstage instead.
