Sea God Scepter

Sealed Artifact A-003, Sailor (Tyrant) pathway, Sequence 3 level

Appearance

A white bone scepter inlaid with blue gems.

Ability

Made from the Beyonder characteristics of the sea god Kalvetua, it gives command over the sea, storms and sea creatures at roughly the level of a Sequence 3 Sea King.

Drawback

It makes the wielder irritable, periodically drains the blood of everything around it, and floods the holder with the prayers of Kalvetua's believers.

Notes

Grade 1 (first appears Ch. 549). Klein used it for his 'Sea God Kalvetua' identity; its holder at the very end is not stated.

Held By

  • Klein Moretti (C-001)
