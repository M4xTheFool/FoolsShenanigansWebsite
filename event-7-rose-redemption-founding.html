Rose Redemption Founding

950, Third Epoch, Pact or alliance, Eastern Continent (Concealed World)

Pact or alliance

What Happened

Realizing that the Primordial God Almighty's will is irresistibly awakening within him, the Ancient Sun God (through his separated persona Sasrir) secretly approaches Amanises to form the Rose Redemption. Their plan: assassinate the Ancient Sun God's main body so he can cleanly resurrect inside Adam as a pure Visionary free of PGA's corruption. Members include the Subsidiary Gods (Amanises, Lilith, Badheil, Salinger), the Kings of Angels (Sasrir, Leodero, Aucuses, Herabergen, Ouroboros, Medici), and Angel-level nobles (Bethel Abraham, Antigonus, Jacob, Kotar)
