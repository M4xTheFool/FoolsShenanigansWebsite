A mixed-race man in his thirties with distinctive dark blue hair and bronze skin. He usually wears a robe decorated with storm and wave patterns.

About

Calculating Tyrant-pathway sailor, undercover Storm church officer, and one of the Troupe's first two guests

Backstory

Alger Wilson grew up a mixed-race islander who faced constant discrimination. He was dropped from the Church of the Lord of Storms' children's choir and served under a violent priest before becoming a Beyonder. The church then sent him into the Sonia Sea as an undercover intelligence officer posing as a pirate, where he killed the captain of the ghost ship Blue Avenger and took the ship. In early July 1349 he was pulled onto the Everstage by accident. His was the stern, seasoned mask Nika grabbed on her first night there, beside Audrey's, and the name that flashed through her mind was Odysseus. He is calm and ambitious beneath a show of irritability, and he overthinks every word of the silent Phantom and its cheerful Prompter. He became one of the Troupe's most useful guests: he traded Alice the Spectator formula, bought the Prompter's potion ingredients for her, and coached the young Sun Wukong more than anyone else. With the Troupe's help he kept climbing the Tyrant pathway. On 25 June 1360, Nika and Max slew the Lord of Storms, the god whose church Alger had served all his life, and the whole Troupe helped in some way.

Abilities

Sequence 9: Sailor (Sailor pathway)

  • Sea Strength: Gains great strength and balance, staying steady on rolling decks and rigging.
  • Swimmer: Holds breath for a long time, swims fast and resists water pressure.
  • Slippery Scales: Grows faint illusory scales that make the body slick and hard to grab.

Sequence 8: Folk of Rage (Sailor pathway)

  • Rage Surge: Anger fuels big boosts to strength, speed and toughness.
  • Raging Blow: Releases built-up fury in one devastating strike.
  • Drunken Fury: Drinking speeds up the build-up of rage, at the cost of judgment.

Sequence 7: Seafarer (Sailor pathway)

  • Navigation: Masters stars, currents, winds and charts, so ships it guides rarely get lost.
  • Weather Sense: Senses coming storms and changes in the sea.
  • Water Spells: Uses water to smother foes, raise waves, wash away filth or mend small wounds.
  • Home Waters: Its water powers grow much stronger while at sea.

Sequence 6: Wind-blessed (Sailor pathway)

  • Wind Control: Commands gusts, pressure waves and binding winds.
  • Windblades: Fires invisible blades of cutting wind.
  • Wind Flight: Hovers, glides and briefly flies, and cushions falls with air.
  • Wind Whispers: Eavesdrops on distant voices carried by moving air.
  • Deep Diver: Dives deep, floats with ease and sees in the dark.

Sequence 5: Ocean Songster (Sailor pathway)

  • Siren Song: Sings to strike the spirit, dazing listeners or stirring awe, rage or grief.
  • Lightning Strike: Calls down lightning that paralyzes, burns and purifies, and runs through metal and water.
  • Sea Dweller: Breathes underwater, endures deep pressure and talks with sea creatures.

Sequence 4: Cataclysmic Interrer (Sailor pathway)

  • Calamity Weather: Raises hurricanes, tsunamis, floods, blizzards and earthquakes.
  • True Flight: Flies freely on the wind.
  • Chain Lightning: Unleashes thunder strikes and lightning that leaps between foes.
  • Storm Veil: Its storms blind sight, senses and divination.
  • Thunderous Roar: Lets out a roar strong enough to capsize ships.
  • Demigod Form: Gains an incomplete mythical form whose scales serve as armor.

Sequence 3: Sea King (Sailor pathway)

  • Sea Authority: Rules a sea region, raising sea levels and huge tsunamis.
  • Command Sea Life: Commands marine creatures and can turn into sea beasts.
  • Ocean Rejection: Makes the sea itself reject chosen enemies.
  • Rain Sight: Sees and senses through rain, clouds and droplets.
  • Lightning Forest: Raises a forest of lightning bolts.

Sequence 2: Profane Presbyter (Secrets Supplicant pathway)

  • Profane Language: Twists the meaning of words and gestures into curses that imprison, separate, corrupt or erase identity.
  • Malice Shackles: Turns a target's own malice, envy or cowardice into chains that bind it.
  • Self-Reference: Draws strength whenever others refer to it.
  • Many-Headed Grazing: Uses the powers of several grazed souls simultaneously.

    After Amanises uses Antigonus to divert the Curtain of Fool characteristics into the Forsaken Land of the Gods, the third transmigrator (Zhou Mingrui / Klein Moretti) perishes upon arrival, triggering the release of the fourth cocoon: 22-year-old Polish stage technician Weronika "Nika" Sparrow. Because she bowed on an empty theatre stage at the end of her luck ritual on Earth, the unsealed 10th Sefirah—the Everstage—claims her before the Celestial Worthy's net can bind her to the Fool pathway. She awakens in Backlund in the body of 18-year-old Lady Viola Veronica Marlowe seconds after a Secret Order strike team (a Marionettist, a Magician, and a Clown) raids Viscount Marlowe's manor for a Sequence 4 Bizarro Sorcerer characteristic. Waking in a pool of blood with her fatal head wound healing and a silver-white silk streak in her red hair, Nika grabs a revolver and shoots the Secret Order Clown dead as the Nighthawks arrive. A few nights later, she repeats the ritual in an empty theatre, bows to the dark seats, hears a single clap, and steps onto the Everstage for the first time as the Prompter—recruiting Audrey Hall and Alger Wilson (Odysseus) into the Troupe
