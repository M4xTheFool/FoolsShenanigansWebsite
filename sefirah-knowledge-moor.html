The Sefirah of knowledge, sought by the Hermit and Paragon pathways.

Above the Sequence

Symbolism
Knowledge, and the madness that comes with knowing too much.
  • Demon of Knowledge: Knows, and grants, any knowledge, always at a price.
  • Arcana of Madness: Knowledge taken from it drives the one who takes it toward madness.

Notes

Full title: Demon of Knowledge, Arcana of Madness (Ch. 1347). Sealed in the Western Continent in an earlier age, like every Sefirah except Sefirah Castle and the Chaos Sea (Ch. 1369). Sequel: tied to the Celestial Master's sect and the Hidden Sage. The symbolism and Great Old One abilities are an interpretation of the full title and the description; the novel never lists them.
