Sequences

Sequence 9: Marauder

  • Theft: Lifts items from pockets, bags and bodies without the owner noticing.
  • Nimble Hands: Gains fast, precise hands and quick reflexes.
  • Keen Eye: Spots valuables, hiding places and openings for a theft.

Sequence 8: Swindler

  • Deceit: Tells persuasive lies that briefly bend a target's thinking toward believing them.
  • Silver Tongue: Gains eloquence and charm that make people listen and trust.
  • Misdirection: Draws attention away from its hands and its real goals.

Sequence 7: Cryptologist

  • Decryption: Breaks codes, ciphers and secret writing with ease.
  • Pattern Sight: Spots hidden patterns, contradictions and clues that others overlook.
  • Flaw Finding: Perceives weak points in people, defenses, rituals and seals.

Sequence 6: Prometheus

  • Power Theft: Steals a Beyonder ability from a nearby target and can use it for a short time.
  • Borrowed Fire: Turns a stolen power against its owner or others while the theft lasts.

Sequence 5: Dream Stealer

  • Intent Theft: Steals a target's thought or intention, so they forget what they meant to do.
  • Memory Theft: Takes memories from a target, learning what they knew.
  • Dream Theft: Steals into dreams to take secrets and ideas.

Sequence 4: Parasite

  • Parasitism: Lodges inside another being to hide, watch through them or control them from within.
  • Worm Avatars: Splits off small worm-like bodies that parasitize others and act as its avatars.
  • Time Worm Form: Becomes a demigod with an incomplete Worm of Time form that is very hard to wipe out.

Sequence 3: Mentor of Deceit

  • Loophole Exploit: Finds and abuses gaps in rules, contracts, seals and even natural laws.
  • Deceive the Rules: Tricks the world's rules into accepting a false situation as valid.
  • Induced Errors: Nudges enemies and their powers into small but costly mistakes.

Sequence 2: Trojan Horse of Destiny

  • Fate Theft: Steals parts of another's fate, taking their luck, role or future for itself.
  • Fate Parasitism: Hides inside a target's destiny, waiting to seize it at the right moment.
  • Fate Swap: Exchanges destinies between two people, or between itself and another.

Sequence 1: Worm of Time

  • Time Theft: Steals moments of time from others, skipping or erasing what they would have done.
  • Time Parasitism: Lodges itself within time and others' timelines, making it extremely hard to kill.
  • Countless Avatars: Splits into many Worms of Time, each a capable avatar.

Sequence 0: Error

  • Authority of Error: Rules over loopholes, mistakes and deceit, making rules and powers fail.
  • Absolute Theft: Can steal almost anything, from objects and powers to fate, time and concepts.
  • Deceiving the World: Fools reality itself into treating false or impossible situations as real.

Above the Sequence: Lord of the Mysteries

A Great Old One holds Sefirah Castle and the Uniqueness of every pathway it gathers.

Symbolism
Mysteries, the gray fog above reality, the Spirit World, and space and time.
  • Lord of the Mysteries: Holds authority over every mystery, everything hidden and everything unknown.
  • Dominator of the Spirit World: Rules the Spirit World and the spirit bodies that drift through it.
  • King of Space-Time: Commands space and time, as the full title on the Second Blasphemy Slate says.
  • Castle above the Gray Fog: Pulls spirit bodies into Sefirah Castle, hears every prayer addressed to it and hides what happens there from divination.
