Salinger's Death

1233, Fourth Epoch, Death, Battle, Advancement, The Southern Continent - Balam Empire

Death, Battle, Advancement

What Happened

Salinger went mad after trying to force a fusion between the Death and Red Priest pathways, he collaborated with Cheek and waged war. He was ultimately killed by the combined forces of the Seven Orthodox Gods and Max. After dealing the final blow, Max took the promised Sequence One characteristic from his corpse and consumed it, becomming King of Angels. Ante also gained favors from all the Seven Orthodox deities.
