Sequences

Sequence 9: Arbiter

  • Authority: Carries a commanding weight that makes people inclined to believe their words and follow their instructions.
  • Combat Training: Gains a sturdier body and solid fighting skills for handling sudden violence.
  • Composure of Order: Stays calm under pressure and instinctively works to uphold the existing order.

Sequence 8: Sheriff

  • Jurisdiction: Claims an area as their territory and senses crime and anything unusual happening inside it.
  • Recognition: After seeing someone in person or in a picture, can mystically sense that person, making fugitives hard to lose.
  • Investigative Instinct: Sharpened intuition and spiritual perception help track suspects and notice clues.
  • Weapon Mastery: Handles revolvers, shotguns and melee weapons with practiced confidence.

Sequence 7: Interrogator

  • Psychic Piercing: Fires spiritual lightning from the eyes that bypasses flesh and wounds the soul within a few meters.
  • Whip of Pain: A thorny current lashes the target's consciousness, wearing down resistance and drawing out the truth.
  • Psychic Lashing: Wraps a weapon in spectral lightning so it can harm spirits and incorporeal beings.
  • Spectral Torture Tools: Summons ghostly instruments that injure the spirit body on contact.
  • Weapons and Demolition: Gains expertise with a wide range of weapons and with explosives.

Sequence 6: Judge

  • Verdict: Speaks rules in Ancient Hermes that everything in the surrounding area must obey.
  • Prohibition: Forbids chosen actions or powers in an area, such as flight, invisibility, teleportation or entering the spirit world.
  • Imprisonment and Exile: Verdicts that pin a target in place, seal off an area, or banish a foe from it.
  • Flogging and Death: Verdicts of direct punishment, from painful spiritual lashes to an outright death sentence against weaker foes.
  • Judicial Presence: An unsettling aura makes people avoid their gaze, and their jurisdiction can grow toward city size.

Sequence 5: Disciplinary Paladin

  • Punishment: Marks a person or thing as an offender, gaining strength and precise, hard-to-dodge strikes against it.
  • Overawing Authority: An aura of dread slows nearby foes and pressures them to bow their heads and obey.
  • Layered Prohibitions: Stacks several prohibitions on one area or target at the same time.
  • Paladin's Discipline: Body, intuition and self-control all strengthen, resisting attempts to pry into their mind.

Sequence 4: Imperative Mage

  • Imperatives: Issues commands that become binding rules on a target's existence and cannot simply be disobeyed.
  • Weaken Mysticism, Strengthen Reality: Suppresses supernatural effects in an area while boosting the mundane, or does the reverse.
  • Contracts: Binds a target's body and spirit body to agreements that follow the world's rules.
  • Deprivation: Strips a target of one chosen power for a time.

Sequence 3: Chaos Hunter

  • Chaos Detection: Senses, tracks and corrects disorder such as curses, possessions, disguises and restless undead.
  • Sword of Judgment: Delivers a near-unavoidable judgment that hits harder the more disorder a target carries.
  • Early Balance: Partitions a battlefield and imposes order on it, though that order can slide back into chaos.
  • Answer Prayers: Can hear and respond to prayers from within a certain range.

Sequence 2: Balancer

  • Balance: Equalizes a conflict by excluding excessive power and outside help, splitting battles into roughly fair fights.
  • Laws: Enacts several decrees at once, applying multiple prohibitions or enhancements instantly.
  • Imbalance Sight: Detects hidden people and tricks through the imbalance they create.
  • Brass Pillar Form: Gains a complete angelic form, a colossal brass pillar inscribed with rules.

Sequence 1: Hand of Order

  • Order Authority: Establishes, alters or removes non-fundamental rules across a battlefield, a city or a small nation.
  • Order Proxy: Borrows authorization from a deity or greater order to enforce rules beyond their own strength, at serious risk.
  • Order Perception: Senses every change in law and supernatural order within their domain and suppresses anomalies.

Sequence 0: Justiciar

  • Divine Order: Lays down rules, removes lesser ones and adjusts how parts of reality operate.
  • Divine Judgment: Decides who has broken the law and imposes the consequences.
  • Absolute Prohibition: Suppresses powers, identities, connections, movement or entire phenomena.
  • Balance and Disorder: Equalizes any conflict and can restore, extinguish or deliberately create disorder.

Above the Sequence: The Anarchy

A Great Old One holds Nation of Disorder and the Uniqueness of every pathway it gathers.

Symbolism
Order and its shadow: laws, rules, and the chaos they hide.
  • Shadow of Order: Twists laws and rules into their opposite, so order breeds disorder.
  • The Anarchy: Brings rules, nations and order itself crashing down.
