City of Calamity

Sefirah

Overview

Great Old One
Calamity of Destruction
Pathways
Hunter (Red Priest), Assassin (Demoness)

With the River of Eternal Darkness it is one of the two symbols of the hidden Fourth Pillar (Ch. 1347). A reflection of it waits behind a door in Bansy Harbor (Ch. 1369).

Above the Sequence

Symbolism
Destruction, disaster and catastrophe.
  • Origins of Disaster: Is the source of disasters and calls catastrophes down on cities and nations.
  • Calamity of Destruction: Destroys on the scale of a natural disaster wherever it turns.

Held By

  • Max (C-016), Sequence 0

Notes

Full title: Calamity of Destruction, Origins of Disaster (Ch. 1347). Sealed in the Western Continent in an earlier age, like every Sefirah except Sefirah Castle and the Chaos Sea (Ch. 1369). Sequel: its will is called the Malevolent Dragon. The symbolism and Great Old One abilities are an interpretation of the full title and the description; the novel never lists them.
