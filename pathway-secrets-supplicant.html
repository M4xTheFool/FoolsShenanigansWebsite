Sequences

Sequence 9: Secrets Supplicant

  • Secret Prayer: Prays to hidden beings for knowledge, rituals or aid, accepting great risk in return.
  • Steady Mind: Resists the madness and corruption that come with forbidden knowledge.
  • Hidden Lore: Instinctively knows about sacrifices, secret entities and the dangerous honorific names that reach them.
  • Spiritual Sense: Senses nearby supernatural auras and can perform rituals and simple divination.

Sequence 8: Listener

  • Listening: Hears the whispers of hidden existences, picking up strange knowledge, rituals and prophecies.
  • Whispered Gifts: Sometimes draws fragments of forbidden power or warnings of danger from what it hears, at risk of corruption.

Sequence 7: Shadow Ascetic

  • Shadow Walking: Merges into shadows to hide, and travels or trails targets through them.
  • Shadow Shaping: Moulds shadows into weapons, beasts or black cocoons that bind victims.
  • Shadow Seizure: Grabs hold of a person's shadow to restrain or harm them.
  • Shadow Curses: Lays curses using blood, flesh or torn fragments of a target's shadow.

Sequence 6: Rose Bishop

  • Flesh Manipulation: Controls flesh and blood, both its own and other people's.
  • Regeneration: Commands its own cells to regrow lost limbs and survive wounds to the heart or head.
  • Flowing Flesh: Melts into a fluid mass of flesh to slip away or hide inside other bodies.
  • Flesh Constructs: Crafts servants, cloaks and bombs out of living tissue.
  • Blood Curses: Curses a target through its blood or tissue, or devours it from within.

Sequence 5: Shepherd

  • Grazing: Devours the souls of the dead or slain and keeps them, using the powers those souls held in life.
  • Soul Flock: Keeps several grazed souls at once and switches between their powers while keeping its own.

Sequence 4: Black Knight

  • Black Armor: Encases itself in black armor and swells into a small giant.
  • Shadow Command: Commands the shadows of a battlefield and binds foes through their own shadows.
  • Black Greatsword: Wields a dark greatsword that corrodes flesh, wounds souls and cleaves through barriers.
  • Thought Peeling: Strips depraved thoughts or invading personalities out of a mind, casting them off as shadows.
  • Betrayal: Makes objects and powers turn against the very people using them.

Sequence 3: Trinity Templar

  • Trinity: Splits personality, body and power into three coordinated aspects.
  • Three Heads: Grows two shadowy heads, one young and one old, each able to regrow into a full being.
  • Single-Cell Revival: Rebuilds from one surviving cell as long as its spirit endures and flesh is available.
  • Shadow Substitute: Lets its shadow take damage in place of its body.
  • Triple Release: Releases up to three grazed souls at the same time.

Sequence 2: Profane Presbyter

  • Profane Language: Twists the meaning of words and gestures into curses that imprison, separate, corrupt or erase identity.
  • Malice Shackles: Turns a target's own malice, envy or cowardice into chains that bind it.
  • Self-Reference: Draws strength whenever others refer to it.
  • Many-Headed Grazing: Uses the powers of several grazed souls simultaneously.

Sequence 1: Dark Angel

  • Shadow Dominion: Rules much of the shadow world and stands firm against purifying light.
  • Degeneration: Makes bodies, items, powers and servants betray and turn on their owners.
  • Sea of Darkness: Summons an ocean of darkness that swallows attacks and dulls thought.
  • Profane Speech: Its words drain life and drive listeners deeper into madness.
  • Depraved Servants: Releases grazed souls as servants that keep their powers but obey through corruption.

Sequence 0: Hanged Man

  • Authority of Sacrifice: Bears others' wounds, death or punishment, or sacrifices itself to secure a desired outcome.
  • Authority of Degeneration: Awakens depravity in the living and corrupts bodies, powers, objects and even principles.
  • Lord of Shadows: Rules the shadow world and spawns monsters from darkness that return after death.
  • Divine Grazing: Absorbs souls, Beyonder characteristics and even higher symbols.

Above the Sequence: God Almighty

A Great Old One holds Chaos Sea and the Uniqueness of every pathway it gathers.

Symbolism
Creation, the source of matter and energy, and the place where the real and the illusory merge.
  • Omnipotent and Omniscient: Sees and can do almost anything within the Chaos Sea's reach, as the full title says.
  • Source of Matter and Energy: Draws matter and energy out of the Chaos Sea to make whatever it needs.
  • Real and Illusory: Merges the real and the imagined, so what it pictures can become real.
