A black-haired woman in a classical black robe covered in mystical symbols, hiding faintly purple eyes behind thick glasses. She carries a small celestial globe and a short scepter at her waist.

About

Pirate admiral of the Future, adopted daughter of Bernadette, and the Hermit of the club.

Backstory

Cattleya's parents are unknown; before she was three she was adopted by Bernadette Gustav, the Queen Mystic, who raised her as captain, teacher and mother. She grew into the Admiral of Stars, commanding the Stars Pirates from her flagship Future, and wore thick glasses to restrain her hard-to-control Eye of Mystery Prying. When the Hidden Sage nearly drove her to lose control, Klein pulled her above the gray fog and she joined the Tarot Club as The Hermit. At Bernadette's request she left her fleet and joined the Moses Ascetic Order, where she came to be counted among its ten Pillars. Letting Gehrman Sparrow board the Future raised her bounty from 37,000 to 45,000 pounds. She quietly became a demigod and, after Mr. Fool fell into his deep sleep, publicly proclaimed herself Queen of Stars.

Abilities

Sequence 9: Mystery Pryer

  • Eyes of Mystery Prying: Sees spirituality, auras and normally invisible things, and spots the flaws in illusions and dreams.
  • Mystical Knowledge: Holds broad occult knowledge, with new insights sometimes arriving unbidden.
  • Quick Rituals: Performs ritual magic, charms and divination quickly and with few materials.
  • Heightened Spirituality: Spirituality rises sharply, fuelling rituals and sharpening intuition.

Sequence 8: Melee Scholar

  • Combat Prying: Studies the principles behind fighting styles and weapons to master them quickly and read opponents.
  • Strengthened Body: Body becomes sturdier, with faster reactions for close combat.

Sequence 7: Warlock

  • Spellcraft: Designs their own spells, such as fire, ice, lightning, wind, barriers or curses, from phenomena they understand.
  • Witchcraft: Brews potions and makes mystical items more effectively than before.
  • Analytical Eyes: Studies blood, remnants and artifacts to extract symbols for new spells.
  • Danger Sense: Greater spirituality gives sharp warnings of approaching danger.

Sequence 6: Scrolls Professor

  • Scroll Making: Stores spells in prepared scrolls, released instantly by reading a word and burning them.
  • Shared Scrolls: Hands scrolls to allies so even non-casters can use spells.
  • Ready Casting: Scrolls let them cast when short on time or spirituality, skipping ritual delays.
  • Scroll Teleport: Reads a prepared scroll to flash a short distance away in an instant.

Sequence 5: Constellations Master

  • Starlight Cage: Brings streams of starlight down on a target to imprison it.
  • Star Pillar: Merges conjured stars into a pillar of radiance that blasts an area.
  • Stellar Self: Dissolves the body into starlight particles to avoid harm and reform through gaps.
  • Star Concealment: Covers an area with stars that block outside sight and interference.
  • Starlight Works: Forms walkable bridges of starlight, lifts ships into the air or lights up darkness.

Sequence 4: Mysticologist

  • Mystical Re-enactment: Recreates effects from myths, legends and secret history; the less known the secret, the stronger the result.
  • Mystical Analysis: Dissects rituals and strange phenomena piece by piece to learn how they work.
  • Deep Prying: Eyes of Mystery Prying now uncover far more hidden objects and properties.
  • Whisper Resistance: Better withstands the maddening knowledge and voices that press on Hermit Beyonders.

Sequence 3: Clairvoyant

  • Prying Authority: Mystery Prying becomes a true authority, reaching secrets far beyond normal limits.
  • Prophecy: Glimpses likely consequences, favorable choices and scenes from the past.
  • Answer Prayers: Can hear and respond to prayers from within a certain range.

Sequence 2: Sage

  • Information Form: Becomes flowing knowledge and symbols, passing through obstacles and rebuilding after partial destruction.
  • Removal and Revision: Deletes or rewrites knowledge, deciding what others are able to know.
  • Teaching: Forces knowledge into a mind; an overload can stun, corrupt or kill.
  • Secret Prophecy: Prying into secrets sharpens prophecy, revealing future fragments as enemies make their moves.

She awakens in Backlund in the body of 18-year-old Lady Viola Veronica Marlowe seconds after a Secret Order strike team (a Marionettist, a Magician, and a Clown) raids Viscount Marlowe's manor for a Sequence 4 Bizarro Sorcerer characteristic. Waking in a pool of blood with her fatal head wound healing and a silver-white silk streak in her red hair, Nika grabs a revolver and shoots the Secret Order Clown dead as the Nighthawks arrive. A few nights later, she repeats the ritual in an empty theatre, bows to the dark seats, hears a single clap, and steps onto the Everstage for the first time as the Prompter—recruiting Audrey Hall and Alger Wilson (Odysseus) into the Troupe
