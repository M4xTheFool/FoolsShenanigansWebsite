Max joins ASG

1342, Second Epoch, Pact or alliance, Eastern Continent

Pact or alliance

What Happened

Max joined the Ancient Sun God and his legion, as a commander and general only below the Ancient Sun God and Medici.
He quickly became really good friends with Amanises. He advanced to Weather Warlock with the help of Ancient Sun God. He was also really good friends with Ouroboros who tells him not to advance to Sequence One "Before the Blood Emperor falls". He became friends with Adam and Amon, with a relationship of mainly mutual respect.
