Max joins ASG

1342, Second Epoch, Pact or alliance, Eastern Continent

Pact or alliance

What Happened

Max joined the Ancient Sun God and his legion as a commander and general, ranked below only the Ancient Sun God and Medici. He quickly became close friends with Amanises, and advanced to Weather Warlock mainly through her: she provided the characteristic, while the Ancient Sun God gave his approval and a place for the ritual, which Max carried out without outside aid. He also grew close to Ouroboros, the weakest of the Ancient Sun God's circle, whom he respected and trained with. Ouroboros warned him not to advance to Sequence 1 "before the Blood Emperor falls".
