Sealed Artifact

Death Knell

Sealed Artifact A-011, Hunter (Red Priest) pathway, Sequence 5 level

Appearance

A revolver.

Ability

Its shots carry the lethal power of a Sequence 5 Reaper.

Drawback

After use the wielder picks up a new phobia or weakness that lasts about six hours.

Notes

In the original novel Klein bought it from Ukfa Connerchris near Bayam and killed Ince Zangwill with it, saying "This shot is for Captain". In this story no one comes to buy it, and it stays in Bayam until Cattleya buys it from Ukfa for the Troupe. Nika makes sure it is in Leonard Mitchell's hand for the last act of her Director ritual. Leonard lands the shot that kills Ince for Dunn Smith. So the gun that killed Ince in the original novel does it again, in the hand of the man who earned it, and he keeps it afterwards.

Held By

  • Leonard Mitchell (C-009)
