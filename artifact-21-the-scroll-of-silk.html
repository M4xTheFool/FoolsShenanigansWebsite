The Scroll of Silk

Sealed Artifact A-021, Acrobat (The Maestro) pathway, Sequence 1 level

Appearance

An ancient scroll woven from shifting black-and-white spider silk, kept sealed in the Backlund main cathedral of the Church of the Evernight Goddess.

Ability

Made from one of the three Sequence 1: Storyweaver Beyonder characteristics. Whatever is written on it plays out as a reasonable development, much like Sealed Artifact 0-08 (Quill of Alzuhod).

Drawback

Causes an immense spirituality drain just from being near it while unsealed. The user must write on it using their own blood, and anything unreasonable written on it causes a terrible backlash to the writer.

Held By

  • Nika (C-011)
  • Amanises (C-014)
