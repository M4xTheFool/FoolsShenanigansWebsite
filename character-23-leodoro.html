A wrathful deity wreathed in apocalyptic hurricanes and blinding spears of lightning, whose mere presence crushes nearby beings into submission.

About

The Sequence 0 Tyrant and Orthodox God of the Seas and Skies, slain by Max and Nika during their 1360 apotheosis war.

Backstory

Formerly the Wind Angel, one of the eight Kings of Angels under the Ancient Sun God. At the end of the Third Epoch, he betrayed the Rose Redemption conspiracy alongside Aucuses and Herabergen, devouring the Ancient Sun God's corpse to ascend to Sequence 0: Tyrant. Throughout the Fourth and Fifth Epochs he ruled the Church of the Lord of Storms, though he was briefly held inside his divine kingdom by Adam when Adam became Half a Great Old One. On 25 June 1360, he was slain in the Northern Continent during the war waged by Max and Nika. Max claimed his Tyrant Uniqueness and one Sequence 1: Thunder God characteristic, while Nika claimed his remaining two Sequence 1 characteristics.

Abilities

Sequence 9: Sailor

  • Sea Strength: Gains great strength and balance, staying steady on rolling decks and rigging.
  • Swimmer: Holds breath for a long time, swims fast and resists water pressure.
  • Slippery Scales: Grows faint illusory scales that make the body slick and hard to grab.

Sequence 8: Folk of Rage

  • Rage Surge: Anger fuels big boosts to strength, speed and toughness.
  • Raging Blow: Releases built-up fury in one devastating strike.
  • Drunken Fury: Drinking speeds up the build-up of rage, at the cost of judgment.

Sequence 7: Seafarer

  • Navigation: Masters stars, currents, winds and charts, so ships it guides rarely get lost.
  • Weather Sense: Senses coming storms and changes in the sea.
  • Water Spells: Uses water to smother foes, raise waves, wash away filth or mend small wounds.
  • Home Waters: Its water powers grow much stronger while at sea.

Sequence 6: Wind-blessed

  • Wind Control: Commands gusts, pressure waves and binding winds.
  • Windblades: Fires invisible blades of cutting wind.
  • Wind Flight: Hovers, glides and briefly flies, and cushions falls with air.
  • Wind Whispers: Eavesdrops on distant voices carried by moving air.
  • Deep Diver: Dives deep, floats with ease and sees in the dark.

Sequence 5: Ocean Songster

  • Siren Song: Sings to strike the spirit, dazing listeners or stirring awe, rage or grief.
  • Lightning Strike: Calls down lightning that paralyzes, burns and purifies, and runs through metal and water.
  • Sea Dweller: Breathes underwater, endures deep pressure and talks with sea creatures.

Sequence 4: Cataclysmic Interrer

  • Calamity Weather: Raises hurricanes, tsunamis, floods, blizzards and earthquakes.
  • True Flight: Flies freely on the wind.
  • Chain Lightning: Unleashes thunder strikes and lightning that leaps between foes.
  • Storm Veil: Its storms blind sight, senses and divination.
  • Thunderous Roar: Lets out a roar strong enough to capsize ships.
  • Demigod Form: Gains an incomplete mythical form whose scales serve as armor.

Sequence 3: Sea King

  • Sea Authority: Rules a sea region, raising sea levels and huge tsunamis.
  • Command Sea Life: Commands marine creatures and can turn into sea beasts.
  • Ocean Rejection: Makes the sea itself reject chosen enemies.
  • Rain Sight: Sees and senses through rain, clouds and droplets.
  • Lightning Forest: Raises a forest of lightning bolts.

Sequence 2: Calamity

  • Disaster Authority: Summons disasters able to wipe out ports, islands or cities.
  • Storm Body: Manifests through vast weather systems.
  • Magnetic Storm: Twists magnetic fields to cut off communications.
  • Source of Wrath: Boosts its power for a while at the cost of reason.
  • Dread Voice: Spreads terror, grief and submission over huge distances.

Sequence 1: Thunder God

  • Lightning Body: Becomes pure energy to move and strike at near light speed.
  • Heavenly Punishment: Calls down lightning that disintegrates and purifies.
  • Storm Hell: Creates lasting storms over cities, islands or seas.
  • Sea of Lightning: Fills a whole area with a sea of lightning.
  • Wrath Surge: Rage briefly lifts its force close to a true god's.

Sequence 0: Tyrant

  • Matter and Energy: Converts matter to energy and back, driving attacks of world-ending scale.
  • Divine Lightning: Strikes with lightning that tears through concealment, darkness, spirits and escape.
  • Tyrant's Presence: Its presence forces fear, awe and submission.
  • Lord of Storms: Oceans, skies, storms and electromagnetic fields carry its will.

  1. 1342, Second Epoch
    Max joins ASGPact or alliance
    Eastern Continent
    With Max, Ancient Sun God, Amanises, Leodoro, Aucuses, Herabergen, Lilith and Ouroboros

    Max joined the Ancient Sun God and his legion as a commander and general, ranked below only the Ancient Sun God and Medici. He quickly became close friends with Amanises, and advanced to Weather Warlock mainly through her: she provided the characteristic, while the Ancient Sun God gave his approval and a place for the ritual, which Max carried out without outside aid. He also grew close to Ouroboros, the weakest of the Ancient Sun God's circle, whom he respected and trained with. Ouroboros warned him not to advance to Sequence 1 "before the Blood Emperor falls".

  2. 2561, Second Epoch

    Following the earlier slayings of Phoenix Ancestor Gregrace and Dragon of Imagination Ankewelt (during which Dragon of Wisdom Herabergen surrendered), the Ancient Sun God slays Elf King Soniathrym and storms the Giant King's Court. Betrayed from within by Badheil and Lilith (masquerading as Giant Queen Omebella), Giant King Aurmir falls. Reclaiming the Sun, Visionary, Hanged Man, Tyrant, and White Tower authorities alongside the Chaos Sea, the Ancient Sun God declares "Let there be light!", ending the 2,561-year Dark Epoch and ushering in the Third Epoch.

  4. 1086, Third Epoch
    The Fall of Ancient Sun GodBattle and Death

    During the Rose Redemption's attack on the Ancient Sun God inside the Divine Kingdom, Leodero, Aucuses, and Herabergen betray the plan! Instead of allowing the Ancient Sun God to resurrect cleanly in Adam, the three Kings of Angels fall upon his corpse and devour his Tyrant, Sun, and White Tower Uniquenesses and Sequence 1 characteristics to ascend to Sequence 0 on the spot. Enraged by the betrayal, the Ancient Sun God's resentment, humanity, and remaining Hanged Man characteristics fuse into the mad True Creator, ruining Adam's immediate resurrection plan (forcing Adam to remain a King of Angels until the Fifth Epoch). The resulting divine cataclysm shatters the Eastern Continent into the Forsaken Land of the Gods, rips open the Abyss and the Sea of Ruins, and leaves the Second Blasphemy Slate o condense from the Ancient Sun God's fallen flesh and blood. Sasrir falls into eternal slumber in the Giant King's Court holding the First Blasphemy Slate
