A river of darkness with many tributaries, running through the underworld where the dead go. With the City of Calamity it is one of the two symbols of the hidden Fourth Pillar.

Above the Sequence

Symbolism
Darkness, death, the underworld, and space and time folded into one.
  • Eternal Darkness: Covers everything in a darkness that swallows light, sound and life.
  • River of the Dead: Rules the underworld where the dead go, through the river's many tributaries.
  • Space-Time-in-One: Folds space and time into a single point, as the full title says.

Held By

  • Amanises (C-014), Above the Sequence
  • Salinger (C-020), Sequence 0

Notes

Full title: Eternal Darkness, Singularity of All, Space-Time-in-One (Ch. 1347). Sealed in the Western Continent in an earlier age, like every Sefirah except Sefirah Castle and the Chaos Sea (Ch. 1369). It was the first to leak, with a distributary since the Second Epoch, and the Evernight Goddess uses its water (Ch. 1379); no one is named as holding all of it. The names Netherworld and Yellow Springs come from the sequel, Circle of Inevitability. The symbolism and Great Old One abilities are an interpretation of the full title and the description; the novel never lists them.
