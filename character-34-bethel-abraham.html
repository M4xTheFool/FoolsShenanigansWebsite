A tall aristocratic gentleman in Fourth Epoch Tudor attire whose body dissolves into a vortex of countless illusory doors and starlight.

About

Backstory

The King of Angels of the Door pathway who was banished into the Cosmos during the War of the Four Emperors to prevent the Mother Goddess of Depravity from descending through him. His ravings during every full moon cursed his Abraham descendants and drove Fors Wall to seek salvation from The Phantom. In Fall 1352, Amon replaced him during his apotheosis ritual to ascend as Sequence 0: Error.

Abilities

Sequence 9: Apprentice

  • Door Opening: Draws a symbolic door on a wall or thin barrier and walks through it.
  • Unlocking: Opens ordinary locks and doors with ease.
  • Slip Restraints: Opens cuffs, ropes and other bindings to slip free.
  • Ritual Basics: Learns basic mysticism and simple ritual magic.

Sequence 8: Trickmaster

  • Minor Tricks: Casts many small spells such as flashes, fog, darkness, freezing, sparks, shocks, gusts and loud bangs.
  • Slippery Ground: Makes a patch of floor slick so enemies stumble.
  • Small Telekinesis: Roughly nudges or moves light objects from a distance.
  • Escape Trick: Chains tricks together to dart over ten meters away almost instantly.

Sequence 7: Astrologer

  • Astrology: Divines through star charts, celestial symbols and crystal balls.
  • Fate Reading: Reads looming fate and danger, with sharper spiritual intuition.
  • Memory Recall: Recovers blurred or forgotten memories.
  • Disguise Insight: Recognizes the same person beneath different disguises.
  • Divination Interference: Disrupts others' divination and intuition aimed at it.
  • Shared Door: Brings companions through walls and opens tiny peepholes to spy through.

Sequence 6: Scribe

  • Recording: Records an active Beyonder power it witnesses and saves it for one later use, weaker if the source is stronger.
  • Recorded Library: Stores several recorded powers at once, with room growing as the potion digests.

Sequence 5: Traveler

  • Traveler's Door: Opens a door into the Spirit World and steps out far away, carrying anyone it touches.
  • Blink: Teleports a short distance in an instant.
  • Spirit World Bearings: Always knows its position and finds its way within the Spirit World.
  • Invisible Hand: Grabs objects from a distance with an unseen hand.
  • Spirit Veil: Partly hides itself by drawing close to the Spirit World.

Sequence 4: Secrets Sorcerer

  • Concealed Space: Creates hidden pocket spaces behind secret doors, used as prisons, vaults, safe rooms or arenas.
  • Secret Keeping: Hides secrets and conversations from divination, intuition and eavesdroppers.
  • Door Body: Turns into layers of illusory doors to slip past attacks.
  • Exile: Temporarily banishes a target into turbulent space.
  • Star Worm Form: Becomes a demigod with an incomplete Worm of Star mythical form.

Sequence 3: Wanderer

  • Astral Wandering: Travels through the Astral World to distant planets and other dimensions.
  • Space Integration: Blends into surrounding space to hide and survive hostile environments.
  • Space Tearing: Rips space on contact, collapsing matter and the area into a spatial storm.
  • Spatial Cage: Turns concealed spaces into prisons that hold powerful enemies.
  • Barrier Breaking: Door Opening can now force through most barriers.

Sequence 2: Planeswalker

  • Planar Travel: Teleports to far planets, dreams, the Mirror World, the Underworld and hidden spaces.
  • Symbol Form: Turns into symbols to cross the Astral World efficiently.
  • Dimension Sight: Views distant scenes across space.
  • Miniaturization: Shrinks people, objects or whole areas into toy-sized spaces.
  • Replication: Reproduces Beyonder powers it has seen without recording them first.
  • Sealing: Seals beings, powers and even concepts, including inside prisons of starlight.

Sequence 1: Key of Stars

  • Starry Gateway: Opens doors from almost anywhere into the Astral World and the starry sky.
  • Stellar Positioning: Pinpoints nearly any place or being while keeping its own location hidden.
  • Stellar Gravity: Uses star-like gravity to bend, crack and crush space.
  • Starry Banishment: Casts enemies into the remote starry sky, far from any easy return.

From the Uniqueness: Sequence 0: Door

  • Supreme Positioning: Finds almost anything while making its own position impossible to locate.
  • Authority of Doors: Governs space, sealing, entrances, exits, boundaries and alternate worlds.
  • Absolute Sealing: Seals, or breaks seals on, beings, abilities, connections and even rules.
  • Instant Passage: Moves instantly between the physical, spirit and astral worlds.
  • Star Gravity: Bends and shatters space, distorts time and redirects light.
