A refined-looking man with black hair, bright red eyes, a high nose and thin lips. He is handsome in a delicate way and is usually seen in formal clothes or a priest's robe.

About

Proud Sanguine vampire, Harvest Church priest and devoted doll collector.

Backstory

Emlyn White is a Sanguine, a member of Backlund's hidden vampire clan, proud by nature and convinced he is the one meant to save his people. While his family was quietly drawing blood from a hospital, he caught a thief and kept the thief's Master Key. The key's curse left him lost and led to his capture by Father Utravsky. Utravsky made him a priest of the Harvest Church, and Emlyn's faith has been slowly shifting from the Moon and the Sanguine Ancestor Lilith toward the Earth Mother. He joined the Troupe after its first year, and the name that stuck to his mask was Lestat. Trading through the Troupe let him advance faster than the rest of his clan, and his Sanguine rank has risen with each Sequence. Away from his duties he likes to stay home, spending lavishly on his doll collection.

Abilities

Sequence 9: Apothecary

  • Medicine Concoction: Brews herbal remedies and potions that cure most illnesses and injuries, short of terminal ones.
  • Poison Craft: Prepares poisons from herbs and has modest resistance to poisons and toxins.
  • Spirit Vision: Sees a person's overall health and well-being at a glance.

Sequence 8: Beast Tamer

  • Taming: Tames, domesticates and commands animals, even Beyonder creatures.
  • Animal Sense: Talks with animals, shares their senses and reads their emotions and intentions.

Sequence 7: Vampire

  • Rapid Regeneration: Becomes a vampire whose wounds close at unnatural speed, making it very hard to kill.
  • Wings of Darkness: Sprouts shadowy bat wings for swift flight, or bursts them into a swarm of attacking bats.
  • Blood Drain: Drinks a victim's blood to sap their life and restore its own strength.
  • Claws of Corrosion: Extends sharp, corrosive nails that cut steel and tear through scales or hide.
  • Abyss Shackles: Condenses darkness into chains that bind and restrain enemies.
  • The Embrace: Converts humans into vampires or into obedient blood servants.

Sequence 6: Potions Professor

  • Specialty Potions: Brews potions with Beyonder effects, such as invisibility, fire breathing, dreamless sleep or shadow cloaking.
  • Material Discernment: Instantly judges the properties of spiritual ingredients and how to combine them.
  • Sun Water: Prepares a holy liquid that badly harms undead and vampires.
  • Cure for the Incurable: Medicines can now treat even terminal illnesses.
  • Physical Enhancement: Gains greater speed, agility and strength.

Sequence 5: Scarlet Scholar

  • Lunar Battlefield: Shapes the area with moonlight, conjuring a full-moon field or a moonless dark that favors the Scholar.
  • Moonlight Flash: Teleports short distances whenever moonlight is present.
  • Moonlight Body: Turns into condensed moonlight that reforms after shattering unless the heart is struck.

Sequence 4: Shaman King

  • Instant Rituals: Performs rituals instantly by drawing on nature, including raising corpses as undead servants.
  • Moon Paper Figurine: Swaps places with a paper figure made of moonlight that takes the hit instead.
  • Gaze of Darkness: Curses a target through a linked eyeball, restraining or gravely wounding them via the eye.
  • Bat Swarm Body: Splits into a swarm of bats and reforms unless every single bat is destroyed.

Sequence 3: High Summoner

  • Door of Summoning: Opens an illusory door to call powerful creatures from the Spirit World and other realms.
  • Mythical Creature Form: Takes a fuller mythical form and can hear prayers offered within a certain range.

Sequence 2: Life-Giver

  • Life Creation: Brings entirely new living beings into existence.
  • Proliferation: Spurs living things around it to multiply and flourish.

    With Nika (Awakens), Klein Moretti (dies), Amanises, Viscount Marlowe, Fors Wall, Audrey Hall, Alger Wilson, Emlyn White, Cattleya, Leonard Mitchell, Xio Derecha, Sharron and Derrick Berg

    After Amanises uses Antigonus to divert the Curtain of Fool characteristics into the Forsaken Land of the Gods, the third transmigrator (Zhou Mingrui / Klein Moretti) perishes upon arrival, triggering the release of the fourth cocoon: 22-year-old Polish stage technician Weronika "Nika" Sparrow. Because she bowed on an empty theatre stage at the end of her luck ritual on Earth, the unsealed 10th Sefirah—the Everstage—claims her before the Celestial Worthy's net can bind her to the Fool pathway. She awakens in Backlund in the body of 18-year-old Lady Viola Veronica Marlowe seconds after a Secret Order strike team (a Marionettist, a Magician, and a Clown) raids Viscount Marlowe's manor for a Sequence 4 Bizarro Sorcerer characteristic. Waking in a pool of blood with her fatal head wound healing and a silver-white silk streak in her red hair, Nika grabs a revolver and shoots the Secret Order Clown dead as the Nighthawks arrive. A few nights later, she repeats the ritual in an empty theatre, bows to the dark seats, hears a single clap, and steps onto the Everstage for the first time as the Prompter—recruiting Audrey Hall and Alger Wilson (Odysseus) into the Troupe
