Backstory

Max joined the Ancient Sun God in 1342 of the Second Epoch as a general under him and Medici, and reached Sequence 2 Weather Warlock mainly through Amanises, his closest friend among the gods. In 1634 the Ancient Sun God gave him a city to rule as a gift of friendship, which Max named Mundi Fracti, the Broken World. Known as the Number of War, he refused promotion and stayed lower and less known than Medici, the Red Angel. Warned by Ouroboros, he waited out the Blood Emperor and advanced to Conqueror only after Alista Tudor fell in 1052 of the Fourth Epoch. In 1233 Amanises asked him to help slay the mad Salinger; Max landed the killing blow, took Salinger's Conqueror characteristic and the favours of every god involved, and became a King of Angels known as the God Killer. During the God of Combat's war with Amanises he spent those favours to make Herabergen hand over the Red Priest Uniqueness, and accommodated it through a simplified ritual: his endless war against the Forsaken Land and its evil spirits. On 25 June 1360 he advanced to Sequence 0 Red Priest alongside Nika. Almost no Angel is completely sane, and Max, after so many epochs and what he has been through, least of all.

Abilities

Sequence 9: Hunter

  • Hunter's Craft: Masters wilderness survival, tracking prey, building and placing traps, and handling explosives.
  • Heightened Senses: Sharper sight, hearing and smell let the Hunter follow faint trails and spot hidden prey.
  • Physical Enhancement: Gains greater strength, speed, reflexes and body control, plus faster natural healing.
  • Danger Intuition: Passively senses hostility and threats aimed at them before they strike.

Sequence 8: Provoker

  • Provocation: Taunts a target with cutting words until they lose composure, act rashly and fixate on the Provoker.
  • Trigger Reading: Quickly spots the sore points, pride and habits that will most easily enrage a given person.
  • Physical Enhancement: Strength, reflexes, speed and agility improve another step.

Sequence 7: Pyromaniac

  • Pyrokinesis: Conjures and shapes fire at will, from compressed fireballs to flame bombs that detonate on a delay.
  • Blazing Spear: Hurls a white-hot spear of flame that pins its target in place and burns it.
  • Fire Ravens: Releases a flock of burning ravens that lock onto several targets and explode on contact.
  • Wall of Fire: Raises a barrier of writhing flame serpents that blocks and scorches anything trying to pass.
  • Flame Armor: Wraps the body in protective fire and sets weapons or projectiles ablaze, harming even spirits.
  • Fire Resistance: Barely hurt by flames, though the force of explosions can still injure.

Sequence 6: Conspirer

  • Conspiracy: Uses misdirection, confusion and deception to steer situations and opponents into prepared traps.
  • Schemer's Insight: Thinks faster and more clearly, reading people and situations to find openings for a plot.
  • Incitement: Plants chosen thoughts or desires in others through seemingly casual conversation.
  • Flame Jump: Merges into a flame or fire spell and reappears wherever that fire is, starting at about ten meters.
  • Crimson Flames: Fire burns hotter and can be shaped into whips and sabers solid enough to parry.

Sequence 5: Reaper

  • Weakness Investigation: Detects a target's vulnerable points, even in barriers and non-physical bodies.
  • Cull: Charges attacks with a life-reaping force that can kill outright when it lands on a weak point.
  • Precision: Guides fireballs, fire ravens and other flames exactly, splitting them to strike several targets at once.

Sequence 4: Iron-blooded Knight

  • Galvanization: Turns the body, and even its flames, into steel at will for tremendous toughness.
  • Chain of Command: Forms a hidden link with subordinates to speak silently, pool their strength and ease their burdens.
  • Mind of Steel: Becomes highly resistant to fear and mental attacks without losing sound judgment.
  • Weapon Augmentation: Makes ordinary objects into deadly, durable weapons that can be hurled or wreathed in flame.
  • Calamity Giant: Can take an incomplete Mythical Creature form, a giant of calamity, at a heavy cost.
  • Masculine Change: The potion turns a female drinker into a man.

Sequence 3: War Bishop

  • War Song: A battle cry that raises allies' morale, strength and stamina while weakening nearby foes.
  • Fog of War: Clouds senses and mystical links within an area, making most curses and divination fail.
  • Ritualist of War: Once per battle, offers sacrifices to ask the essence of war for aid, such as swapping positions.
  • Army Unleashed: Command over troops deepens, letting soldiers move as one and fight at their full strength.
  • Prayer Response: Hears and answers prayers within range, growing stronger the more soldiers anchor it.

Sequence 2: Weather Warlock

  • Weather Manipulation: Calls up or calms storms, blizzards, lightning and rain over a vast area, even triggering quakes or tsunamis.
  • Wind Flight: Rides the wind to fly and hover freely.
  • Charisma: Naturally inspires loyalty and admiration without needing to charm anyone.
  • Meteor Flames: Rains violet-blue fire down like a meteor shower across tens of kilometers.
  • Calamity Giant Form: Becomes a complete giant of calamity that sets onlookers ablaze and brings storms with it.

Sequence 1: Conqueror

  • Conquest: Radiates overwhelming supremacy that subjugates people, objects and even terrain into its soldiers.
  • Conqueror's Armor: The steel form becomes iron-black, blood-stained armor rivaling the toughest Angels.
  • Shared Rank: Soldiers under its command can borrow its rank, powers and spirituality, at a steep cost.

Sequence 0: Red Priest

  • Authority of War: Embodies war and the hunt, spreading conflict and destructive urges and ruling every soldier who fights.
  • Destruction: Destroys physical and even conceptual things, able to collapse space-time with a clenched fist.
  • Divine Conquest: Conquers anything, even abstract ideas, and forms a divine kingdom from conquered land.
  • Masculinity: Holds authority over male forces and can turn beings or objects male.
