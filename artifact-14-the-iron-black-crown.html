Sealed Artifact

The Iron-Black Crown

Sealed Artifact A-014, Hunter (Red Priest) pathway, Sequence 4 level

Appearance

An iron-black crown, unadorned with any jewels. Sharp iron spikes protrude from it both up and down every couple of centimeters.

Ability

It gives physical enhancement of a sequence 4: Iron-Blooded Knight. It also gains the strengthened Cull ability.

Drawback

The blood draws large amounts of spirituality every minute in use.

Notes

Can be activated or deactivated even while being worn. To activate you pre-emptively load spirituality for the first minute, then it just continues draining every minute after it. For sealing it requires being sealed in the spirit world or a realm adjacent to it, so it can draw upon the energy of the realm to satiate itself - it will start draining after not being fed at all for 24 hours.
It has been given to Nika by Max as a gift and token of goodwill.
Made from the characteristic of a Broken World soldier, an Iron-Blooded Knight of only 17, whom Max had taken a liking to. The boy died long ago in an attack while Max was away. Max kept the crown in his vault until he gave it to Nika around her Sequence 5.

Held By

  • Nika (C-011)
