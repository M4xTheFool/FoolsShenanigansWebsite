Symbolism
Evil, desire, curses and everything deviant.
  • Source of Curses: Curses are born from it and answer to it.
  • Lord of Deviants: Rules devils, the depraved and everything that strays from what is right.

Notes

Full title: Father of Devils, Lord of Deviants, Source of Curses (Ch. 1347). Sealed in the Western Continent in an earlier age, like every Sefirah except Sefirah Castle and the Chaos Sea (Ch. 1369). Sequel: tied to the Shadow Cottage sect. The symbolism and Great Old One abilities are an interpretation of the full title and the description; the novel never lists them.
