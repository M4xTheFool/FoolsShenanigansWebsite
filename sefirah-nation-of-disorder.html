The Sefirah of the Black Emperor and Justiciar pathways. It corrupted the Justiciar Uniqueness, which became the Trunsoest Brass Book (Ch. 1288, per the wiki).

Above the Sequence

Symbolism
Order and its shadow: laws, rules, and the chaos they hide.
  • Shadow of Order: Twists laws and rules into their opposite, so order breeds disorder.
  • The Anarchy: Brings rules, nations and order itself crashing down.

Notes

Full title: The Anarchy, Shadow of Order (Ch. 1347). Sealed in the Western Continent in an earlier age, like every Sefirah except Sefirah Castle and the Chaos Sea (Ch. 1369). Sequel: the Heavenly Court, whose will is called the Celestial Thearch. The symbolism and Great Old One abilities are an interpretation of the full title and the description; the novel never lists them.
