The Sefirah of the Mother and Moon pathways, torn from the Mother Goddess of Depravity's own Sefirah (Ch. 1271, per the wiki). The Mother Goddess of Depravity is one of the Three Pillars (Ch. 1347).

Above the Sequence

Symbolism
Life, birth and growth, and the filth and decay that come with them.
  • Mother of All: Gives birth to and shapes life of every kind.
  • Brood Hive of Filth: Spreads corruption, rot and decay through living things.

Held By

  • Mother Goddess of Depravity (C-030), Above the Sequence

Notes

Full title: Goddess of Origins, Mother of All, Brood Hive of Filth (Ch. 1347). Sealed in the Western Continent in an earlier age, like every Sefirah except Sefirah Castle and the Chaos Sea (Ch. 1369). Sequel: its will, the Unborn Ancient Mother, is worshipped by the Luo Sect. The symbolism and Great Old One abilities are an interpretation of the full title and the description; the novel never lists them.
