Creeping Hunger

Sealed Artifact A-001, Secrets Supplicant (Hanged Man) pathway, Sequence 5 level

Appearance

A thin black glove of human skin with a hidden maw in its palm.

Ability

It devours the souls of Beyonders and keeps them inside, letting the wearer borrow their powers. These match Sequence 7 to 5 Hanged Man pathway abilities, such as driving targets mad, entering dreams and changing appearance.

Drawback

It has to be fed. While sealed it eats a soul each time it is used; otherwise it wants a living person's flesh and soul every other day, and a starving glove will turn on its own wearer.

Notes

In this story no one warns Azik. Audrey still spots Qilangos, and her warning still reaches Alger, through the Prompter instead of the Fool. But Qilangos gets out of Backlund alive with the glove. Later it comes to the Prompter Nika uses it for a time, then passes it to a Troupe member
