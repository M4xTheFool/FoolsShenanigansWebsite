A Nation-City in the Forsaken Land of the Gods, far from the Giant King's Court: mostly a city, but a nation for the purposes of Conqueror rituals. The Ancient Sun God gave it to Max in 1634 of the Second Epoch, soon after Max joined him, as a gift of friendship, and Max named it Mundi Fracti. At first it was home to all species, under one core law: everyone is equal under the law, whatever their species or gender, so everyone gets the same opportunities. Now only humans remain. Because Max has conquered the land and everyone in it, the Forsaken Land's curse can't claim them: they don't mutate, their dead don't rise as evil spirits, and no child has to kill their parents. Food comes through Max's collaboration with Amanises. Its people are an army of capable soldiers from many pathways. Max fights alone and won't let them watch; they fight only when he's away, under his Right Hand Man, Renji. Nobody in the world talks about it, because of its ruler.
