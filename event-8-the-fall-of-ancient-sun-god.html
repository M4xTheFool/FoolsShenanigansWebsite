The Fall of Ancient Sun God

1086, Third Epoch, Battle and Death, Eastern Continent (Becomes the Forsaken Land of the Gods)

Battle and Death

What Happened

During the Rose Redemption's attack on the Ancient Sun God inside the Divine Kingdom, Leodero, Aucuses, and Herabergen betray the plan! Instead of allowing the Ancient Sun God to resurrect cleanly in Adam, the three Kings of Angels fall upon his corpse and devour his Tyrant, Sun, and White Tower Uniquenesses and Sequence 1 characteristics to ascend to Sequence 0 on the spot. Enraged by the betrayal, the Ancient Sun God's resentment, humanity, and remaining Hanged Man characteristics fuse into the mad True Creator, ruining Adam's immediate resurrection plan (forcing Adam to remain a King of Angels until the Fifth Epoch). The resulting divine cataclysm shatters the Eastern Continent into the Forsaken Land of the Gods, rips open the Abyss and the Sea of Ruins, and leaves the Second Blasphemy Slate o condense from the Ancient Sun God's fallen flesh and blood. Sasrir falls into eternal slumber in the Giant King's Court holding the First Blasphemy Slate
